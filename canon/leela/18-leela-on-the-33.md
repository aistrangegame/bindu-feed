## How it plays
- Intention first (Gaia's ask), and a paradox toggle: **My life** / **What I'm building** — the same board read for life or for the build.
- 33 squares from The Map (Book Position order): 1 Bindu · 2–8 Energy (Muladhara → Sahasrara) · 9 Shadow · 10–19 Body (Soles → Crow) · 20 Armin · 21–29 Mind (Inception → Integration) · 30 Maya · 31 Aatma · **32 held (sealed; no content)** · 33 Lalita. Each square carries its question, verb, signature line and a short practice from The Map. No seed syllables.
- Start unborn at 33 ("Who is playing?"); a six → born onto 1 and throw again; exact landing on 33 to arrive; overshoot → the die rests.
- **Arrows (first draft, for Ashrey's ruling):** 5 Anahata → 8 Sahasrara (the heart's gesture returns at the crown) · 12 Prana → 18 Axis (the breath's open door becomes the blink) · 13 Hands → 20 Armin (love leaving through the hands arrives whole) · 26 Invention → 29 Integration (what assembles, integrates).
- **Snakes (first draft):** 15 Sonar → 6 Vishuddha · 19 Crow → 10 Soles · 28 Innocence → 21 Inception · 30 Maya → 9 Shadow. A snake is a lesson: its move is the square's practice, made small. An arrow's move is a small act of giving.
- **Living reading (Claude, quick tier):** a reading of ≤60 words meeting the intention through the square, one move ≤20 words, one seed line ≤12 words; laws in every prompt (never score, judge or predict; no urgency; offer, don't command; care line if crisis). At 33, the walk is read back (Sakshi's naming). Without Claude: the square's own practice, verb and line.
- **Own palette and dot:** pale stone ground (#E3E9EA; dark mode slate), squares in each position's own Map colour, peacock dot #0F7C7A, gold arrows, violet dotted snakes. Lora + DM Sans (kin to the ASG chassis). State kept on the phone (`asg.leela33`); no counts shown.
