# comps/ — the per-register source material (subordinate to the Instrument)

These are the individual per-screen / per-register HTML comps and their JS content that the
unified **`../The Instrument v3.html`** was assembled *from* (The Point v9, The Universe v2/v3,
The Light v2, The Return, The Rite v3, The Gathering v3, Game View, Story Detail, The Mirror,
The Signal Space, Room Selection, Ash's Voice/Compose, plus `point-content.js`, `point-levels.js`,
`uni-*.js`, etc.).

**Precedence:** `The Instrument v3.html` is the blessed unified design and **supersedes the
per-screen look here**. Use these comps only for single-register detail the unified Instrument
compresses. Where exact wording or numbers matter, `../../canon/` wins over both.

`point-content.js` in this folder is the verbatim upstream of `../../canon/point-content.js`
(the 66 Point stars / 7 dimensions / 22 universes).
