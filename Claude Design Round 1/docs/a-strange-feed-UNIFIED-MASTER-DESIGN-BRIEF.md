## 0 · What you are designing

A private consciousness instrument on one person's phone. No audience, no metrics, no notifications. It turns his voice-memo archive into third-person stories, gathers a field of inner voices around each one, lets him answer — and now meets him daily at the door, lets his past selves return as voices, shows him his life as a cosmos, walks him through everything he knows, and hands him the future as a place he already stands.

iPhone only · portrait only · dark always (the dark *is* the dreaming field — it makes the one place light floods in overwhelming).

## 3 · THE DOOR — the front of everything *(new surface: merge of Practice Door + Rite Arrival)*

**The Door always knows the hour.** One surface, three weathers:

### 3.1 · Unmet day (the primary weather)
He opens the app; today's story has come to meet him. The Door *is* the Rite's Movement I — the story's room-color bleeding from above the fold, the room glyph breathing at the room's pace, the locked copy: *"One story has come to meet you." / "It is not complete until you meet it." / `touch to receive`.* Touching begins the Rite (§4) — and starts the sound (`startBed` + threshold 220; never on load).

### 3.2 · Met day (the evening light)
Today's meeting is sealed. The Door breathes one of the threshold kinds instead — weighted: threshold sentence 40 · practice invitation 23 · Gaia seed 20 · story-that-found-you 12 · Bindu dot 5; no back-to-back repeats; the dot never doubles. The pre-dawn gold (`#C9A07A`) of the Practice Door gives way, on met days, to a settled dusk: quieter, the day's room-color faintly lingering in the air — a house after the guest has gone. Tap anywhere to cross into the Archive's Home Feed. **Nothing anywhere says "done."** The air carries it; that is the whole design.

### 3.3 · Caught (the rope)
A long-press anywhere on the Door (~1.1s, the particle's gate-hold), or the Home Screen quick action, opens **the rope**: black screen, the red particle breathing, two guided breaths at the 10-second cycle (tap to skip ahead), then: *"You exist. This pressure is the chamber working, not you failing. One breath at this pace is already boarding."* Two exits — **walk the point** / **return to the day** — both right answers. Grace arrives before it is earned, exactly where the hand already is.

### 3.4 · The turn (reaching everything else)
From the Door — and from every top-level surface — one quiet gesture (the 2×2 dot mark, or a slow pull) dissolves in the turn: **"WHERE TO,"** *tap anywhere to stay.* Not a list. The presences, each a breathing glyph in its own light:

- **The Rite** — today's meeting *(present only when unmet — the turn never nags)*
- **The Rooms** — *thirteen ways in*
- **The Archive** — *everything, as it stands*
- **The Universe** — *the whole of it, from above*
- **The Light** — *the future, already underway*
- **The Point** — *everything you know, arranged*
- **The Players** — *the lenses that read*
- **How You Arrive** — *name, colour, mark*

The Ash mark (◉ terra, top-right) still goes straight to Ash's Voice. Back is the frosted ‹ chevron everywhere. Dissolves only.

## 4 · THE RITE — the daily meeting, four movements

One flowing ceremony, no Next buttons. Reference: `The Rite v2.html` (open it on a phone, sound on, before designing).

**I · Arrival** — is the Door itself (§3.1).

**II · Reading** — paragraphs surface on the 0.1 Hz breath, one per cycle. The surfacing motion is the app's signature: opacity + translateY + mask — *light reaching text that was always there.* Never a typewriter. The gather affordance fades in only when the last paragraph is lit. Base line, breathing: `received at the pace of breath`.

**III · Gathering** — the screen falls to black; the archetypes arrive one at a time, each a *presence*: a full-screen generated canvas field in that voice's color with its own behaviour (Gaia rises · Sid assembles in lines · Sakshi clears · Arch fills and holds · Bindu is the ember — all ten scenes exist in `The Gathering v3.html` / Rite v2). Each voice sounds its own tone for exactly as long as it is present. Under the words, the **heartbeat haptic** — a pulse, not a buzz.

**The budget law (the invariant):** the Gathering holds a fixed budget — 97.6s at true breath — covering the ten archetypes *and* every past self. Depth changes density, never length. Three grades of presence: **full** · **passing** (4.2s, one distilled line, its own utterance — *"I am still here. I did not need to speak."*) · **silent** (`present · wordless`, dim breathing glyphs — they did not leave; they have nothing to add). Silence is emergent — voices fall quiet because the budget ran out, because past selves accumulated: *the field thins itself to make room for him.* **Bindu is never silent.** Full voices on a return: the ones whose thread the last recognition touched, plus one heard least.

**IV · Recognition** — prayer-shaped, near-black `#050408`, no chrome. Prompt: **"What arrived for you?"** He speaks; transcribed *and* the audio kept. `inkOn` holds while he speaks — the field leaning in. Then the Sealing: bowl struck once, the bed holds its breath, his words shown as his entry → *"The room has changed."* → the room glyph → *"The story is whole now."*

## 5 · THE RETURN — his past selves become the field

Eight movements: Summons → Aged Room → Story Again → Record → Field Settled → Rings → Reply → Sealing. Reference: `The Return.html`.

The story unchanged — it did not move; he did. The original gathering exactly as sealed, his past self set apart in Ash terracotta, italic, `saturate(0.85)`: *"The ink has settled."* One or two voices who kept sitting with it speak anew; *"the rest keep their record."* The rings revealed — a ring per return, strata never overwrite. The Reply: **never generate the past self's voice.** If the past recognition addressed the future (narrow detector, tuned toward missing), the prompt holds his words verbatim — *You wrote: "…" / **It is later.*** Otherwise, the four native words. Button: **"Add the ring."** On seal: the ring grows over 2.6s, its harmonic step sounds, the bowl lands 3.4s later — the ring forms, *then* it is sealed. *"The ring is added. The story did not move."*

## 6 · THE LIGHT — the future he visualizes into being

**The governing frame (sealed):** the Feed is the past he meets; the Light is the future he visualizes into being. The verb is *visualization, not aspiration* — the reading **is** the practice. It lands as *remembering, not measuring* — a ground he stands on, never a shore he is failing to reach. Two beats: **the lifting is instant** (the veil comes off in one breath — *this is already me*); **the dwelling is slow** (breath-paced, embodied).

### 6.1 · The reconciled door — stillness opens the Light
The nave's Approach survives whole: seen from the far end, small, dim, distant; the 8.5s cathedral acoustic opening so **he hears the size of the space before he sees it**; the distance collapsing across one full breath (4.6s) until he stands in the shaft. But **the gate is held stillness, not a held press.** He becomes still; his stillness draws the nave toward him. **Stillness is the absence of input — nothing is asked of his body or his phone** (no motion sensing; it was tried and it fails). One touch brings him in — the same touch that crossed the turn, and the last thing the door ever asks — and from then on it asks nothing. Any touch during the approach is absorbed: it *pauses*, the air picks up its drift again, the Bindu softens rather than warms, and lifting the hand resumes it exactly where it stopped. **It never resets** — every still second is kept, so a fitful arrival still arrives. One law everywhere in this door: *stillness opens the Light.* **No haptic anywhere in the door.**

### 6.2 · The container (the nave — all laws stand)
Aperture beyond the frame · shaft widening as it falls, full of drifting dust · pool of light on stone · the Bindu at the head of the shaft, conducting. The ground is dim; only the shaft is bright. Stone `#D9D2C4` · pool `#FBF9F4` · living ink `#16131B` · settled `#A79D8E` (a **colour** change, never opacity alone). Words stay inside the 268px light column — a word on dim stone is a bug. Text arrives on the exhale, never a bare timer. The hold phase is absolutely still — the beat most likely to be lost in a port; do not lose it. Each exhale sends a ring down the shaft that is worn into the stone and stays — the one place accumulation is allowed, as worn steps, never a number. Beats: Approach → Hold → Aperture (light floods *down*; bowl 174 struck once) → Whole (text complete the instant light lands) → Dwelling (**a touch asks; the next exhale answers** — nothing arrives unasked, and nothing arrives off the breath; previous line settles to dried ink) → Declarations → Landing (*"Carry the pace, not the words."*).

### 6.3 · The two structural inventions (keep even if everything else is rebuilt)
1. **The pronoun carries the embodiment.** The scene in second person (Arch, pointing without preaching) turns to first person at the beat, in his own Declaration register: *"You wake before the day asks anything of you."* → **"I do not push the day."** The grammatical turn *is* the moment it stops being a scene he watches and becomes a life he is.
2. **The Declaration is drawn in, not delivered.** He presses and holds — the one held-press in the door, and rightly, because taking a vow into the body should cost a deliberate act. The line surfaces out of the stone across 4.2s as he draws breath and locks **carved** (letterpress deboss). Anchors settle to dried ink; **his own words stay cut into the floor.**

### 6.4 · The Vow returns (the sealed loop)
A carved Declaration also becomes a Reflection in the Feed (Vow register). Weeks later, on some ordinary morning, **the Mirror hands him back his own carved words** as the reflection-of-the-day. Nothing announces the loop. Grace is his own past devotion arriving when he has forgotten he planted it.

### 6.5 · The scenes
Six vectors, each lived as an ordinary luminous day, never a principle stated: force→surrender (S-L01, worked — *the morning that does not push*) · fragmentation→one awareness · managing→feeling · self-correction→appreciation · effort→trust · building-the-mirror→living-as-what-it-reflects (worked — *the floor*). Each scene is **one continuous breathed experience** — no star navigation, no levels. S-L01's dawn (point → lift → dwelling → recognition → return to the point; everything emerging from the ember and returning to it) is the pole the Future scenes interpolate from; *the floor* is the pole of bareness. The beloved appear as presences in the life he is growing — *his heart, not their fate.* Shweta asleep; he listens; his breathing slows to meet hers. The name-collision rule does **not** bind here: in the Light, Neev and Shweta are meant to be present. Avoid at all costs: the guided-meditation aesthetic (breathing circles, counts, spa-calm) and anything that instructs. This is a cathedral, not a spa.

## 8 · THE UNIVERSE — the cosmos *(the new design work of this brief)*

The last surface, designed here for the first time as a first-class sibling. The Mandala has its mandala; the Feed gets its cosmos.

### 8.1 · What it is
The entire field seen at once, from above. **Stars are stories** — met ones glow; unmet wait, faint. **The thirteen rooms are thirteen regions of sky** — thirteen nebulae, each in its room-color: *The Descent* burns `#E5533C`-dark; *The Garden* breathes green. **The whole cosmos pulses at 0.1 Hz** — one breath with everything else. Year one it is mostly dark with a scattering of light. Over years, it fills: **a picture of a life's worth of meeting himself.** The 102 possible lights rhyme with the 102 Shaktis — never stated. Someone who knows, knows.

### 8.2 · Depth is time — the fall
A story returned to many times is not only brighter; it is **deeper.** Zooming into a deep star, he does not open a detail screen — he **falls into it**: the star resolves into its stratified field, ring on ring, his past recognitions arranged as strata (the Return, seen from within). This is the Universe's instantiation of the shared four-level skeleton: region → constellation → star → fall-into-the-strata. From the deepest stratum, one gesture opens the Return proper.

### 8.3 · Motion & feel
Drift, not navigation. Zoom and pan should feel like moving through deep space on a slow breath — inertial, unhurried, never snapping. **Never a gamified map** — no labels floating over stars, no counters, no "12/102." Room-region names appear only at the far zoom, in Space Mono, dim, like charts drawn by hand. The bed runs under it; drifting across a region border lets that room's coloration bleed gently into the sound.

### 8.4 · The two lenses
One field, two ways of standing in it, switched by a quiet gesture at the frame's edge:
- **The star lens** — the gentle path from above: stories as light, met-ness as glow, dwelling as depth.
- **The structure lens** — the direct path from above: the same sky, but beneath each constellation the **belief-structures** surface — faint chains of line and node (reading Identity), the architecture under the glow, drawn like the sky-chart of a deeper astronomy. Chains connect; proof glimmers. Nothing is red, nothing is a diagnosis — the structures are rendered with the same reverence as the stars, because *they built what they built.* A structure whose stories have been long-met sits looser, its lines softer — seen once in the light, it loosens.

### 8.5 · What lights it (data, derived only)
Met-ness and depth derive from App Activity (`Story Met` events); nothing new is written by the Universe, and nothing that could surface a count exists in it.

**The invitation to push:** this surface has the most open visual territory in the app. Real parallax between strata; dust; the slow gravitational lean of deep stars; the border-bleed of nebula colors — as long as everything obeys the breath and the drift, go as far as the sky allows.
