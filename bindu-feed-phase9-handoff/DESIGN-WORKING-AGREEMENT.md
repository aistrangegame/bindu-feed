# Working agreement — A Strange Feed

## Never act on defaults or timeouts
When I ask questions, I must **wait for the user's real answers**. If a question form times out or returns defaults, I do **NOT** proceed on those defaults. I re-present the questions and wait. The user has been explicit and repeatedly frustrated about this — it makes them "run for no reason." No exceptions.

## Slow down
Move deliberately, one considered piece at a time. Don't rush ahead, don't fire rapid test/screenshot sequences, don't barrel into the next task. Give each piece full attention and intention. Pause for the user's read before moving on.

## Decisions that are "material" (always ask, never assume)
Navigation structure, new sections/screens, content, and anything that ripples across the app or into the Claude Code build. Ask first.
