The Sound

the whole app, one instrument · comp 7

Touch once to wake it. Then walk the rail: eight places, each with its own bed. Moving between them is always a crossfade, never a cut. Press any event to hear it in place.

The waveform is the real output on an AnalyserNode — so you are seeing the thing you are hearing, which is the point of Snd.breath() being readable by the visuals.

Ruling 1 · the Rite Hz table

Pitch is the Rite / Instrument v3 table. Timbre is field-sound's CHAR. They were never in competition — one is a pitch set, the other a voice set, and the build collapsed both.

Why: the Rite table draws every voice from the ladder the instrument already climbs — gaia 174 · sakshi 285 · lalita 396 · karishma 528 are rungs of FREQS; bindu 136 is OM. One tuning system for the whole app. field-sound.js:27 is a musician's set (A2, E2, D3, F3, A3…) — lovely intervals, but it makes the field a separate instrument from the axis it lives on. It is also the older document.

But field-sound.js:13-25's CHAR is the only per-presence timbre spec that exists anywhere, and it is superb: Neev's partials [0.5,1] and 4.4s attack, Shweta at gain 0.012 with a band of air, Arch's vib 4.6, Bindu's flicker 6.2, Karishma's shimmer, Lalita's gliss 1.02, and eleven distinct pan positions from −0.4 to +0.4. Keep every value. A/B it on the Rite and hear what the collapse cost.

Ruling 2 · the bed

Both. They are different rooms, and the app needs both: the field's bed is root + fifth because it is a room you are standing in; the Point's bed is a binaural pair because it is a state you are being moved into. BEATS belongs to the Point alone.

Reading them as rivals is the error. field-sound.js:53-70 — root, fifth at 0.16, lowpass 900/Q0.6, LFO on the room's own breath — is architecture: it tells you the size and warmth of where you are. point-sound.js:42-58 — two sines at f and f + beat panned ∓0.6, plus the octave at 0.06 — is entrainment: 8 → 4 Hz, alpha into theta, which only means something on a ladder you are climbing. The Point climbs; the field does not.

The eleven Rite events, and the one that matters most

The stillness gate is the one place in the app where sound answers the absence of a hand.

E4.2: thin makes no sound at all in the build. Everything else in the instrument responds to touch; this one responds to your having stopped. It should be the most distinctive sound in the app and currently it is silence. It is on the axis, third from the top — press be still and then do nothing.

Two rules the whole layer obeys

Felt, not heard. Master ceiling is deliberately low; no event exceeds 0.075. Muting is a fade, never a cut — the ◍ at top right takes 0.5s, and prefers-reduced-motion suppresses events while leaving the bed.
