⚠ What the map revealed

No voice has ever returned to a story twice. Building the map surfaced it: across all eleven rooms, every voice has spoken at most once per story, so every cluster at depth 1 currently holds exactly one comment. The legend says so in the frame — 3 stories · none twice — and the word returned appears only when a story actually holds two or more. The structure is built for returns and the archive does not have any. Either the record is thinner than the design assumed, or returns exist upstream and were never ported — worth settling before launch, because “the stories it keeps returning to” is one of the three things you asked a room to show.

The map shows the gap honestly. Lalita's stats say 31 fields; three are in the record. So the map draws 31 marks and lights the 3 — the dim ones are real positions with no words behind them. Ashram's 117 days do the same. Nothing is invented to fill the figure.

The law

A room is a voice's own mathematics at four registers — and the way the figure answers your hand is that voice's own gesture.

All eleven figures are ported from rite-scenes.js, where they were authored as "not decoration laid over a colour, but the mathematics the vantage IS." They were Rite scenes: a 30%-height panel, once, over in seconds. Here they are the room's body. What is new is scale, permanence, the hand, and register 3.

The eleventh room is different in kind

Ashram has no figure in rite-scenes.js — and he shouldn't. He is "not a lens. He is the one the lenses read." So his mathematics is the only one that isn't a geometry: it is time — 117 days as a spine, flecked where a voice spoke on that day. At register 3 the ten figures appear around him, all facing in. That is his turn: being read.
