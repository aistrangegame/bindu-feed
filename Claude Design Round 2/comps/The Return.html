2 · The write-back — and the register-2 answer

You said it plainly: a comment left in the Rite or the Return goes back, and voices may answer. That is the mechanism register 2 was missing.

A voice's second comment on a story is not a repeat. It answers what you left there.

So the depth-1 cluster is not a list of duplicates — it is a conversation across time. Lalita on C-1052 three times: once on the story, once answering your first return, once answering your second. Each answer is bound to the ring it replies to, which is why returns generate genuine new material instead of noise.

That is also why the archive currently shows no voice returning twice: the loop has never run. Nothing is missing from the record — the record is exactly one pass old.

⚠ One design rule I have changed, on your instruction

The Return v2.html:359-360 states the rule: "It writes nothing back — a ceremony is not a place that keeps score." The write-back contradicts it. I think the rule survives intact if read precisely: what is written back is a return, not a score. No count is displayed anywhere in this comp — return-depth is only ever strata, patina, and the thickness of a cluster. Nothing here counts; everything deepens. But the rule's text must be updated, and Claude Code should not discover the contradiction on its own.
