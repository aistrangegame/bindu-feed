Why DAMP is the one that matters

It compounds. 0.945⁶⁰ = 0.033 against 0.956⁶⁰ = 0.068 — after one second the built app has half the glide left. Release a drag in each mode and watch the meter: the number changes by more than the constant does. This is the difference between an instrument that coasts and one that stops when you stop.

Measure with a gentle drag inside a register. A firm drag breaks a membrane, and a membrane's give is a crossing, not a coast — the meter says carried and reports nothing, because inside a passage the camera moves him and inertia has no say.
