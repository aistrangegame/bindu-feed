The Seam

one continuous descent · comp 1 of the upgrade

The audit's finding, rendered: the sky, a region, a world and the fall are not four screens. They are four reading distances on one z, and the object you touch never changes.

What this comp is authoritative for

continuityOne camera, z 0.22 → 34. A star's radius is R = pr · z — the point of light at the sky grows into the planet you land on. Nothing swaps renderers. bands() overlap; they never switch.
the doorA world offers its story by turning its name into the light. The name rides the sphere: you can always see where it is — a bright point — but it is only legible, and only a door, on the day side. Drag sideways on a world and you turn it — the design's own law (vertical walks the axis, horizontal is the register's own gesture) arriving at the distance where the register is one body. Its own spin is 90–290s; the hand never waits for it. An unmet world offers nothing. The story surface itself is out of scope here — the door proves a world can offer its story, not what opens.
consentIn the fall, pulling up descends. The mouth opens only while the hand keeps asking: the ring closes as you pull, and stops the instant you stop. Release and the mouth breathes and holds — it never fires by itself, and it carries the story with it.

What it is deliberately not

Region armatures, the thirteen region forms and the planet's civilisation signatures are already verbatim in UniRegions.swift and drawPlanet. Placement here is schematic on purpose. Do not port this file over those — keep them.

As built

Toggle it. Stars stop growing, the four distances become three hard cuts, the world loses its door, and the fall becomes a pinch-scrub that fires the Return without asking and drops the story on the way.

Silent by design — the eleven sound events are a separate pass, after the bed is ruled.
