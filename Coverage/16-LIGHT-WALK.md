## Why three checks were not reached, and what was done about it

No dissolve scene was in today's draw (the seven Essence scenes are 708–714; none was drawn).
The plan allows seeding the day's draw **in the simulator, never in the base**, so
`bindu.light.draw.2026-09-21` was rewritten to `company · echo · doorway · crow · hold · hole` —
`company` (712, `recGJPACSwNNX2hUu`) carries the dissolve wash, the particle-and-space material,
a blank carving AND a `[none]` landing, so one scene answers B6, B8 and B9 together. The app
was terminated and relaunched; **the seeded six were restored and stood** (B10 again).

**Then the instrument failed, not the app.** The five dawn points drift on the breath at
≈2.4 pt/s (`LightPlaces.place`: `a = t·0.043`, radius `0.29·W`) against a 30 pt hit radius
(`LightPlaces.hit`), and the screenshot→tap round-trip through the simulator tool ran 15–60 s
under a machine load average that reached **295** — two booted simulators and two other Xcode
sessions building at the same time. Every tap on a drifting point missed by more than the
radius. The static Far one was the only point reliably tappable, which is why the walk got its
nave evidence and not its dissolve evidence.

**That is §10's own rule** — *when several unrelated checks fail the same way, suspect the
instrument before the code* — and the honest record is that B6/B8/B9 remain UNSEEN, not that
they passed. They are the next thing to walk when the machine is quiet. The seeded draw is
still in the simulator's defaults, so the retry needs no setup.
