# 2 · MECHANISM COVERAGE SWEEP — the check no tool does

Generated by enumerating every named function, method and state variable declared in
`design-source/*.js` and the seven `comps/*.html`, then asking of each whether an app
analogue exists. **485 behavioural declarations** in scope (the 670 local state variables
in the same files are listed separately in `Tools/mech-state.json` and were not swept).

## Why this file exists

The other four checks all key on something a mechanism might not have:

| check | keys on | blind to |
|---|---|---|
| `check_authored.py` | authored strings | a mechanic with no words |
| `check_rendered.py` | rendered strings | a mechanic with no words |
| `reconcile_audit.py` | AUDIT.md findings | anything the audit did not notice |
| `check_citations.py` | doc citations | anything nobody cited |

A behaviour with no string and no audit finding is invisible to every one of them. That is
how `TURN IT`'s three-face world turn and the whole `#carry` system stayed hidden for the
entire build. **This sweep keys on DECLARATIONS**, so it can find a missing mechanic that
never had words.

## Headline

**83 of 485 design mechanisms are ABSENT from the app. 74 more are PARTIAL.**

- **PORTED** — 288  (59%)
- **PARTIAL** — 74  (15%)
- **ABSENT** — 83  (17%)
- **N-A** — 40  (8%)

Total swept: 485

`N-A` is browser-only plumbing with no native meaning — canvas DPR sizing, `requestAnimationFrame`
handles, DOM class toggles, comp instrumentation rigs that live outside the phone frame.
Every `N-A` carries its reason in the evidence column.

## The five findings that matter most

**1 · The seven register laws of the Point are entirely unsounded.**
`narrow` · `widen` · `unveil` · `bear` · `reflect` · `nul` · `distance`/`send`/`arrive`/`arriveAll` ·
`join`/`ensemble`/`leaveAll`/`dancers` — thirteen mechanisms, **zero implemented**.
`PointReadings.swift` and `PointWorlds.swift` contain **no `soundEngine` calls at all**.
Each law is that register's whole claim expressed as physics: the Mirrors' inverted second
tone, the Chamber's room ringing under load, the Dance's five real voices finding lock. The
design's own header calls sound *"the only layer that can prove the whole thing is one body."*

**2 · Every world declares a leaving decay. Six of seven are absent; not one of the seven
closed lines ships.** `world-one`'s `leaving`, `world-two`'s `reeling`, `world-three`'s
`closing`, `world-four`'s `easing`, `world-five`'s `settling` (partial — and its `given`
reset is missing), `world-six`'s `home`, `world-seven`'s `lock`. This confirms and extends
the suspicion that prompted the sweep: it is not four worlds, it is all seven.

**3 · World VII has no dance.** 11 of its 23 mechanisms absent — no `chain`, no `hand`, no
`offer`, no `letGo`, no Kuramoto coupling, no `lock`. The caption at `PointReadings.swift:984`
prints `\(revealed) hands` — **it counts hands that do not exist.**

**4 · There is no mute.** No `setMuted`, no `setOn`, no sound control in Settings. And
`darkReturns` is absent, so `lightVeilLift` drains the bed on the way into the Light and
nothing refills it on the way out — **you leave the app quieter than you found it.**

**5 · `renderAnswers` is absent.** A return you seal is never answered by a voice.
`sealReturn` writes the ring and your words and stops. This is the mechanism that makes
returning generate new material rather than a second copy of the same page.

## One reversed, not missing

`world-three.js:97 hold` — the Veil gives its sections on **release** where the design gives
them **while the parting is held**. The exact inversion of *"each section must be read THROUGH
a parting he is actively holding."* Not a gap; a reversal, and no string could report it.
