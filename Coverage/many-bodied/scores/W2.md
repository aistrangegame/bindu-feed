**Gesture (touch) — the wash.** A slow **thumb circle** anywhere: angular progress is accumulated around the running centroid of the touch path; each full turn (2π) advances wash `w` by 1/6. `.slosh` on every full turn. Taps and straight drags do nothing.
**Stillness twin.** `w` advances 1/6 for every 5 s of stillness.
**Motion.** As `w` goes 0 → 1: the pairs cross-fade stiff → soft **in order of appearance**, each over 1.6 s, at evenly spaced `w` steps; the typography relaxes with `w`: weight medium → regular, tracking −0.01 → +0.005 em, jitter → 0, grain → 0, line spacing × 1.00 → 1.08.
**Every visit starts stiff.** The text is the same each time; nothing is gained by coming back ("the many-hundredth time is the first").
**No pairs?** The typographic softening alone carries it.
**Sound.** When `w` reaches 1: `fieldThreshold(hz: 146.67, dur: 3)`. Nothing per turn.
**Logic.** Done when `w` = 1 and 6 s pass, or the closing dot is tapped.
**Memory surface.** Softness (§6.4).
**VoiceOver.** Step action "wash" (advances `w` by 1/3). VO reads the **soft** text throughout (the body).
**One-line form.** The same, over one line.
**Release (wring).** On leaving, the text skews 0 → 4° → 0 (skewX) once in 0.8 s and fades — wrung out.
**Room test.** **No.** The Forgetting also clears an overlay as he stays, but it removes a *costume* to show a bare moment, by time alone; the Wash changes the **words themselves** from stiff to soft, by the hand's circular washing, and begins stiff again every time — renewal, not removal.

### 5.6 THE NAME · say · trace — "say it"
**Who speaks.** Names before names: a word for space hidden in a family name, a child's shout that named a company, one cloth with five names. People appear only as the meanings of names (the privacy law made beautiful).

**Scene.** The title becomes the stage: Lora 40 regular, tracking 0.12 em, centred, each letter drawn **unspoken** — stroke only, `c` 0.12, no fill.
**Stillness — say it (three breaths).**
1. after 2.5 s still: the **initials** (first letter of each word) fill to 0.95, 0.6 s each;
2. after 2.5 s more: the remaining letters fill left → right, 0.08 s apart;
3. after 2.5 s more: the **meaning** fades in beneath (`⟪meaning:⟫`, else the landing line), Lora italic 17, `c`.
**Touch twin — trace.** Drag over the letters: each letter whose glyph bounds the finger passes through fills (0.3 s) with `.letter`; tracing ≥ 70% of letters completes the word phase; the meaning follows 1.2 s later.
**Other names.** If `⟪names:⟫` is present, after the word phase the title cross-fades through each name (1.8 s each, 0.6 s cross-fade) and comes to rest on the true title.
**Then the story.** The body arrives beneath, unit by unit (dwell or tap), Lora 18.
**Sound.** Each initial: `blip` on 220 · 247.5 · 275 · 330 · 366.7 in turn (pentatonic on 220, cycling). The meaning: `riteBowl(hz: 220)`.
**Haptic.** `.letter` per traced letter; `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator(.soft)` 0.3 at the meaning.
**Memory surface.** The letters he traced (§6.4).
**VoiceOver.** Step action "say" (completes initials → word → meaning). VO reads title, meaning, body.
**One-line form.** The single line is the meaning; the title does the three breaths, and the line arrives as the meaning.
**Release.** Title letters unfill right → left, 0.06 s apart, then fade.
**Room test.** **No.** No other room makes the **title** the stage, or begins a story as letters assembling into a word and then into what the word means; the Body traces *in the dark under the text*, the Name traces the *letterforms of a name*.

### 5.7 THE OLD LIGHT · watch · let pass — "the light already left"
**Who speaks.** The cosmos before Earth; the FlAshes; myths longer than one life. Cosmology only as rhyme, never stated as fact; no Strange Story exact lines.

**Scene.** Ground `#05060C`, 70 stars (as §3.2). Each unit first appears as **points**: every word a 1.5 pt dot in `c` at its laid-out position. Then the light **arrives**: each word resolves dot → glyph with a delay proportional to its distance from the top-centre of the screen (0.04 s per 10 pt; ~2–3 s per unit). Past-tense units rest in `c` (#CFE0FA) at 0.82 — old, cool light.
**The last line is now.** The final sentence is rendered in warm `inkPrimary` at 1.0 and appears **instantly**, with no travel.
**Watch (stillness, primary).** Units arrive by stillness: next after `dwell(current)`. **Let pass:** as each new unit arrives, earlier units drift up 12 pt and dim to 0.45 (still readable).
**Touch twin.** Tap advances; a slow upward swipe lets the current unit pass.
**Sound.** Bed Shimmer 110. Each unit's arrival: `blip(hz: 880)` at its quietest. The last line: `nul(secs: 4.4)`.
**Haptic.** Only at the last line: `.present`.
**Memory surface.** Star trails (§6.4).
**VoiceOver.** Step action "let pass". VO reads plainly; the last line is announced after a 0.5 s pause.
**One-line form.** The line's words arrive as travelled light, all but the last clause; the last clause arrives instantly, warm.
**Release.** Text drifts up 20 pt dimming over 1.4 s.
**Room test.** **No.** The Signal is light from far off arriving *toward* a question; the Old Light renders **tense** itself — what he reads is drawn as light that already left, and only the last line is now. No other room changes tense by rendering.

### 5.8 THE FOLD · turn over · leave folded — "inside the question"
**Who speaks.** The shirt sewn but not yet tested; the swatch in hand; the one who hasn't turned it over. His own pending moves rhyme here.

**Scene.** The story reads normally, unit by unit (dwell or tap), until the **test**: the units after `⟪underside⟫` (else the last unit) are on the **underside** of a fold. The bottom third of the page is folded up behind (its back face `c` 0.16, a soft crease shadow 6 pt). At the end of the visible text the flap breathes: lifts 3° at its edge on a 6 s sine.
**Two full endings.**
- **Turn it over (touch).** Drag up from the **bottom edge** (start within 44 pt of the bottom, any x), ≥ 40% of the screen height: the flap rotates down about the crease (rotation3D x-axis 180°, 1.1 s); the underside shows the test. `.flip` at the midpoint. Short drags spring back 0.4 s.
- **Leave it folded (stillness).** Still for 12 s after the last visible unit: the flap settles flat (breathing stops), its crease glows `c` 0.3 for 2 s. **Received, complete.**
Both call `done()`. Both are full endings; neither is a miss. Turning stays possible on every later visit (the story reopens on its fold) and "read it plainly" shows everything.
**Sound.** Turned: `fieldThreshold(hz: 146.67, dur: 3)`. Left folded: `nul(secs: 4.4)`.
**Memory surface.** Doors left ajar (§6.4).
**VoiceOver.** Two actions: "turn it over", "leave it folded". VO reads the visible text; after either action, VO reads the underside too (the story's text is always reachable).
**One-line form.** The line splits at its last comma; the last clause is the underside.
**Release.** The bottom third folds up again (0.9 s) as the page fades.
**Collision note.** The story-page **Turn** (§7) is a page curl from the **bottom-right corner**. The Fold's fold is a drag from the **bottom edge** and lives only in reception; Turn is live only on the received page. They never share a surface at the same time.
**Room test.** **No.** The Signal is *before* the question; the Fold is *inside* it, one beat before the test, and it is the only room with two endings, one of which is not to turn. A Maya Game turns over a page to its other side; the Fold may choose not to.
