## 0 · Purpose and feel

**What this wave is.** The Feed stops being one app among six and becomes the place where every app's moments turn into light. App Activity — the shared ledger — is read as **conditions that light, never lock**; the Feed writes its own acts back to it so the other apps can do the same. The Field's songs become the stories' songs. A verb can be asked. Words he offers anywhere sit in one sky. Seen from far enough away, the whole house is one figure.

**What Ashrey should feel.** He felt a Shakti in the Mandala yesterday; today, in the river, one story is a little warmer and he does not know why. He walked a room in the Chakras app; the Door's ground is tinted the colour of the room that rhymes with it. He danced *still* last week; when he opens a story that carries that song, one quiet line in its margin says so. He holds the bead *lets go* and a story lifts, and under it the song that rhymes with it already has its question waiting. Nothing announces any of it. Nothing tells him an app did something. **The Done line is the whole wave:** *a moment in another app changes the Feed's light the next time it opens, and nothing announces it.*

**What this wave adds no story text to.** The only new on-screen words are a handful of labels and composed margin lines (Appendix D), each registered.
