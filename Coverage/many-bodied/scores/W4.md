## 0 · What this wave is, in one breath

The house gets a day and a season. A morning that asks **one** thing and lets silence answer. A Rite that opens on the line he sealed last night, draws one story from the whole river, and can be walked again with any story. A board of thirty-three on which a held throw lands in a room and that room's story answers. A deck and a die. An evening Return that can meet any story — sealed or not — shows the story's other tellings as strata, lets a later memo write a coda onto an old story, and offers a possible echo of something he once asked. And a season: a letter in grace shape, every title said back one breath at a time, a name that surfaces with the eyes closed, seven gates to set things down in and seven gifts on the way up, and a wager that comes back on its own to ask *did it appear?*

**What Ashrey should feel.** Nothing announces the round. The Door one morning simply asks how yesterday's story sat; another morning it asks nothing. One evening the Return opens on a story he never wrote a word under. On the first morning of winter a letter is on the floor of the Door. He never sees a number, a streak, a "day n", a "you completed". Every move has a still way through. On a heavy day the house asks him nothing at all and opens with who he is.

**The hard laws for this wave** (CONTRACT §7, GAMEPLAN §2):
- **Nothing counted.** No die face as a numeral, no square number, no "n of 33", no "3 days left", no count of rings, titles, wagers or gates. Brightness and position only.
- **Lit, never locked.** Every square, every gate, every season letter, every wager, every face of the Return is reachable now. Conditions change light, order and which thing speaks first.
- **One thing at a time** (GAMEPLAN §2 law 11, plan review N7). The Door shows its weather plus **one** ask or arrival per opening; everything else is a Turn row away.
- **Never on a low day.** The ask, the wager's return and every arrival stand down. The rooms, the board, the gates and the Return stay open. The Rite opens with *who he is*.
- **No mechanism words on screen** — never "board position", "square", "chakra", "vertebra", "wager", "stitch", "mode", "season letter", "telling", "ink", "relay", "baton", "low day", "Leela". The Turn rows name places (`The Board`, `The Draw`, `The Gates`, `The Return`), as `The Rooms` already does.
- **Sound**: two voices at most (the Breath + one transient), ceiling 0.55, nothing before the first touch or first stillness threshold, every new pitch on 110 × a simple ratio or on 136.1. The Recitation is read, never spoken (narration is held).
- **Accessibility**: every gesture has a stillness twin and a named VoiceOver action; Reduce Motion keeps order and hold length; every text reachable by VoiceOver at every moment.
- **Sealed stays sealed.** Square 32 shows nothing, ever. No Strange Story exact lines (the stitch questions are written new, §3.5 E). No Sakshi exact sentence — the relay shows *his* line only (§3.5).
