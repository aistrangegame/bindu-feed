### The lines (as stored)

**13501 · Small Enough to Hold**  
It came in an envelope, no bigger than her palm.  
She rubbed me between two fingers and turned me in the light.  
I was small enough to hold, and true enough to stand for the whole yard.

**13502 · Everywhere Was Already Where**  
A child pressed the doll's dress on crooked, then anywhere at all.  
It stayed, every time, wherever her hand came down.  
You were looking for the right place. Everywhere was already where.

**13503 · The Water They Did Not Take**  
Upstream, they found a gentler way to rinse the colour through.  
Some of what they used to draw from me, they simply left.  
I was the water they did not take. I went back to the river instead.

**13504 · Somebody Asked Me**  
Dust settled on my cylinder, and the lights came on only some days.  
Then someone walked in carrying a question instead of an order.  
Nobody bought me. Somebody asked me, and I ran.

**13505 · The Only Answer I Need**  
I never meet the ones I sew for.  
I cut, I stitch, I fold it into a box and let it go.  
Sometimes a picture comes back of someone asleep under it. That is all the answer I need.

**13506 · I Just Kept Arriving**  
No letter came with me, and no review.  
Only the same address on the label, winter after winter.  
Nobody wrote a story about me. I just kept arriving.

**13507 · What Their Life Sounds Like**  
Nobody planned me.  
Named, begun, carried, handed on, each step the same stride apart, like stones across a river.  
I am what their life sounds like from far away.

**13508 · All of Me**  
I am hummed at every gathering, and almost no one knows my words.  
One night a man danced only to my silences.  
That was the first time anyone heard all of me.

**13509 · The Dent in the Pillow**  
Every guest leaves a dent in the pillow and a cup beside the sink.  
I let them stay until morning.  
That is how I remember who was home in me.

**13510 · Monday Again**  
Out in a small backpack, home by the week's end, into the water.  
Every wash I come back a little more theirs.  
Monday again, and I am the first one ready.

**13511 · So the Makers Could Pick Them Up**  
Once I sold finished things beside the makers who bought my cloth.  
I let the last of them go and did not make more.  
I set the finished things down so the makers could pick them up.

**13512 · The Review Never Written**  
She used it every day and never once wrote about it.  
No stars, no sentence, no thank you.  
I am the loudest thing she ever said about the cloth.

**13513 · Both Ways Through the Same Hands**  
She learned to sew for the smallest ones.  
Now she pins a pattern for the ones who first taught her.  
I run both ways through the same pair of hands.

**13514 · Until I Was a Door**  
For a long time I only took.  
Letters went in, and nothing came back out of me.  
Then someone answered me, one at a time, until I was a door.

**13515 · Where I Work**  
Hold me to the window and you will see the street through me.  
People think the openings are where I am missing.  
The openings are where I work.

**13516 · Weather on a Sheep's Back**  
Snow on the ridge, the flock moving, nobody hurrying the cold.  
Before I was soft I was weather on a sheep's back.  
Whatever warms you now came down off a mountain first.

**13517 · After I Had Said It**  
A sentence was half-formed, and one word in it was already shining.  
I did not choose it. I noticed it after I had said it.  
So did he.

**13518 · Beneath His No**  
Every shopping trip had ended in the car, empty-handed.  
Then one shirt went on without a word from him.  
It hadn't won him over. It had arrived beneath his no.

**13519 · Slow Down and I'll Go**  
I fed the mesh fast, and the machine jammed.  
I fed it slowly, and it went all the way through without a pucker.  
It had been teaching me its pace the whole time.

**13520 · Still on Its Way**  
I keep every light that ever left you.  
Some of it is still travelling toward someone who has not looked up yet.  
Nothing you shone has finished arriving.

**13521 · Different Houses**  
In one maker's notes I am the thing that didn't work.  
In another's, I am what finally did.  
Neither of us is wrong. We were only standing in different houses.

**13522 · I Know the Way Now**  
The first time, it took a long week to rise.  
The second time, only a few days.  
I am the same fire. I just know the way now.

**13523 · Enough to Begin**  
They did not decorate me.  
Over a long weekend they hosed me down, swept the sawdust out and left the door open to dry.  
They washed me. That was enough to begin.

**13524 · The One Who Needed It Too**  
He played me until I wore a groove in him.  
He thought it was my sound he loved.  
It was the one in me who needed what he needed.

**13525 · By Morning We Had Doubled**  
I am a bowl left on the counter overnight, a cloth laid over me.  
Nobody looked at us all night.  
By morning we had doubled.

**13526 · In Its Way**  
A spill spread across the floor toward the drain.  
A soft square lay down in its way and drank it before it reached me.  
Long ago someone refused to pour into me. Now their cloth refuses for me.

**13527 · Eyes Closed**  
He touched me in a dim room with his eyes closed.  
Then he said the name of the machine I was born on.  
I had never told anyone.

**13528 · Also Listening**  
I tucked a small square under the invoice, the colour she had mentioned once in a note.  
She opened the box to see if I had kept my promise.  
The square was there to say I had also been listening.

**13529 · Every One of Them Is Right**  
I am the first word anyone says when they touch the cloth.  
In each hand I mean something a little different.  
I never correct them. Every one of them is right.

**13530 · The Iron Above Me**  
She held the hot iron above me like a question she was afraid to finish.  
For a breath, neither of us moved.  
What she feared I would become, I never was.

**13531 · The Last Layer**  
Skin, then the breath under it, then the slow weight under the breath.  
Under all of me, a floor that has never once been in danger.  
Lie down on any layer and you are resting on the last one.
