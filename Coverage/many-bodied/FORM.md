## 0 · How to use this file

- **Rank.** House laws (`Bindu Feed/CLAUDE.md` §10, as amended by the Staged law) › `CONTRACT.md` › **this file** › the wave's score › `GAMEPLAN.md`. Where a score and this file disagree, this file wins, and the score is wrong until it is folded.

- **How it is folded.** Three folding agents apply §5 to the scores, wave by wave: each `F-nnn` names its target score section (or "new in W<n>"), the change in implementable terms, its priority and its source lens. Once folded, the score and this file agree, and the score's text carries the detail. `MUST` = before that wave's build starts. `SHOULD` = in that wave, unless a written DECISIONS line defers it. `LATER` = after delivery, or in W8 if time allows.
- **Lens tags.** `A` architecture (01) · `S` scene (02) · `G` gesture and motion (03) · `W` words (04) · `E` engineering (05).
- **What it is not.** It adds no new content, no new rooms, no new Airtable types. Every place, act and story the scores designed is kept. This pass gives them **one map, one look, one hand, one voice and one machine**.

## 1 · First principles — the laws the whole house obeys

Twenty-one laws. Each one is binding on every wave; each names its why and its lenses. They do not repeat the house laws (CONTRACT §7); they are how the house laws become form.

1. **One place, many faces.** Every surface is one of three things: a *register* of the one axis; a *face* of the one board (River · Sky · Cloth · Arc · Spine · Book); or a *ceremony door* that returns him to exactly where he stood. Context travels with him: the story, the day, the room he was looking at go with him across faces. *Why:* GAMEPLAN §3.2 says six faces of one board; the scores built six islands. *(A P1, P7)*
2. **Lit, never locked — on the map too.** Every place is in WHERE TO on every day; every star, room and row is enterable. Light, form and order say where he is and what the hour offers; nothing is filtered, greyed out of reach or hidden-until. *Why:* law 2; W0 14.5's own fix, made general. *(A P3, S 6)*
3. **One channel, one meaning.** Hue = place (a room, and the presences who are its kin). Material = who tells (paper · linen · stone). Bone thread + mark geometry = rhyme. Light, by form and contrast = relationship and status. The red point = the One (one per screen). Gold = his own hand. Paper = the record. Ground lightness = depth on the axis. Ground chroma = the house's warmth. Motion = the kind of place. *Why:* if two systems share a channel, the eye cannot tell which fact it is reading (§3.1 lists the 14 collisions the scores made). *(S 1, 13)*
4. **One source for every value and every word.** Colours, grounds, type, durations, curves, hold lengths and on-screen/VoiceOver strings each live in one theme or words file; facts live in one owner (a story, a day-key, a room's identity, what he did). *Why:* 114 colour literals, ~40 durations, nine authors of VoiceOver, five duplicated contracts already in CLAUDE.md §10. *(S 2, W §4.1, E 1)*
5. **Readable text is full ink; colour never travels alone.** Text is `ink.1`, `ink.2` or `ink.3` (each ≥ 4.5:1) or it is decoration. A room hue always rides with its glyph, figure or name, never alone. *Why:* `inkTertiary` (2.81:1) carries 41 readable lines in the scores; seven room pairs collapse for colour-blind eyes. *(S 7, 8)*
6. **Dark always; paper is a held sheet.** The ground is never pale except the Light (canon) and LalitaPlay (designed). Paper appears as a sheet held on night, with night around it, and it dims after 21:00. *Why:* the phone is read in low light; six scored full-screen cream pages would flash at night. *(S 5)*
7. **Every inward pass has its outward pass.** ‹ always does something, retraces the way in, and is never a no-op or a jump to a generic home. Leaving is one gesture everywhere, never punished, within a thumb's reach. *Why:* rupakarshini law 3, padakarshini G38, ceremony grammar D8. *(A P6, G P10)*
8. **The map fits one hand.** WHERE TO fits one screen (≤ 700 pt tall at 390 wide; scroll is a fallback, never the plan). The rope is one move from anywhere; any place two moves (WHERE TO → its row); any story three (by sight or by a word). The most-used doors sit in the lower two-thirds. *Why:* 24 rows in a non-scrolling column put Rest, Players and FORGET ME off the glass. *(A P4, P9; G §4.8)*
9. **Every move has its equal paths.** Touch · stillness · a VoiceOver action · a visible, focusable twin on the same surface. A hidden gesture is never the only way; every place is reachable without motion, sound or a long hold. *Why:* law 7; the rope, the Call and the house-as-figure had no visible door. *(A P5, P15; G P9)*
10. **A press means what the thing pressed is; a motion means one thing.** Text → keep; a card → lift; a star → thread; empty ground → rest. One vertical grammar: up a little into the dot = carry; up and out of frame = let go; sinking = lay down; a slow pull from the top = WHERE TO; a firm pull = outward on the axis. Rightward = backward. *Why:* rising-and-fading meant *keep* in the Gates and *let go* in the Unwinding. *(G P2, P5, §3.13)*
11. **Holds come from meaning, run on one engine, and act on the lift.** A new hold takes its meaning's length (§3.6); an existing designed hold keeps its own. At the threshold only the visual and the haptic latch commit; sound, writes and navigation wait for the lift. Holds drain, never fail. *Why:* 22 hold lengths, commit tones that break G0, three engines. *(G P3, P4)*
12. **Readiness may be sensed; crossing is chosen.** Stillness may reveal, lean and carry *inside* a surface he has touched. Stillness never starts a ceremony, crosses a threshold, or completes a write that says he did something (`Story Met`, `Ceremony Sealed`, `Circuit Closed`, `Wager`, `Sent Back`, `Reach`) unless a touch has landed on that surface since it opened. *Why:* a phone left face-up could cross the Door, run the Rite, seal it and log `Story Met`. *(G P7; padakarshini law 15; the truth floor)*
13. **One thing at a time, on the exhale.** One arrival at an opening; one line at the river's head; one after-thing at a story's close; one stillness completion per exhale. *Why:* law 11; the white below a story held eleven clocks firing together. *(A P12, G P6)*
14. **Nothing slides.** A change of place dissolves (0.5 s, with the bloom beneath). Movement within one continuous place is a camera (the axis, the zoom). The two meet only at the named hinge. Reduce Motion lives in the motion tokens, never in a view. *Why:* CLAUDE.md §4/§9; the app honours Reduce Motion in no view today. *(G P11, P12; S 14)*
15. **Discovered, not instructed.** No tutorial and no legend. Each holdable or movable thing looks so at rest, shows its track the instant it is pressed, and, when he is still, leans — performs a fifth of its own gesture — until his hand has done it once. Authored cues are said once and withdraw. *Why:* law 9; CLAUDE.md §10's eighteen owed world cues. *(G P8, W 5)*
16. **The house speaks with one voice, in every channel.** The tense is *already*; person is fixed by who speaks (chrome → *you*; a body → *I*; his memo → *he*; the dot → *I*); invitation, never instruction; silence is complete, never apologetic; one name per thing; no mechanism word on screen **or in VoiceOver**. *Why:* the app has one VoiceOver label today; the scores add ~140 in six grammars, five of which break their own bans. *(W 1–14)*
17. **Nothing counted, in any channel; density is light.** No numeral about him or a story, on screen, in a VoiceOver value or in a hint. At 469 stories crowding is answered by alpha, lanes and proximity, never by a number or a "more". *Why:* law 1; `III · 3 of 7` still stands. *(W 4, S 13)*
18. **Every place moves as what it is; no ending repeats.** A room is known by its tempo and its figure before its name; motion signatures are unique; siblings, host and twin never end alike; each sound answers a hand. *Why:* laws 13, 16; six room exits "rise and fade". *(S 9, 10; G P13, P14)*
19. **The quiet state comes first.** The low day, the empty river and the first open are designed before the rich state. Nothing stands in front of the day's meeting (no lobby). The low day is known **at the opening**, not after he has pulled a hidden rope. *Why:* law 5; the architecture made him declare the low day before it could be honoured. *(A P10, P11; S 15)*
20. **The device is the house; the network only refreshes.** The Door, the river and every story page draw from a local snapshot in under a second with or without signal. One door each to the network, the disk and the clock. Views observe only what they read; frame-rate work lives in leaves and only while seen. *Why:* ≈3,700 rows, 27 sequential pages per story open, 46 publishers re-rendering 45 files. *(E 2, 3, 4, 6, 10)*
21. **Nothing he gives is lost, and every gate is a calibrated mechanism.** A write is durable before it is sent, never doubled, never orphaned; failure is soft to him and visible to the builder. Every rule that matters is a checker with bad, good and absent fixtures, run red before it is trusted. His words never leave the device except to his own base. *Why:* his authored words are the only content that cannot be regenerated; every checker in this build shipped first with the fault it was built to catch. *(E 7, 11, 12, 14)*

### 3.7 Stillness — twins, accumulators, the exhale queue, the touch budget

`Store/StillnessConductor.swift`, one per surface, on `Breath.exhaleDelay(fromPhase:)`.

1. **Two kinds.** A *twin* is stillness since the last touch (a touch resets it) — the equal-path completion of an act. 2. **Ready, then spoken on the exhale.** A twin whose threshold is met becomes *ready*; ready twins resolve **one per exhale**, at the next crest-to-exhale (floor 0.24 s), in ladder order. Two things never move at once on a still screen.
3. **The ladder at a received story's end:** (1) the grammar's last step → (2) the close shape (Arc-kind or silence) → (3) the dealt signature's dwell event (second reading, grace, kept mistake) → (4) **the conductor's one after-thing** (Will → Action, *or* the wager, *or* dance it, *or* journey, *or* held, *or* the draw path, *or* Beat's warmth on pulse rooms) → (5) the reverse dissolve (only after a gathering) → (6) the Turn's lean, then the Turn. ### 3.10 Sound and haptics

#### 3.11.2 AirtableClient
An actor; its methods are `nonisolated` async and decode inside it (nothing decodes on main). **Token bucket 5 requests/s per base, burst 4, ≤ 4 in flight, shared by reads and writes.** 429 → stop the lane for `max(30 s, Retry-After)`, then resume (no exponential storm). 5xx/network → reads retried 3× with jitter (0.5 s, 2 s, 5 s), then the snapshot serves. Identical GETs in flight are coalesced (single-flight). 401/403 → `AuthState.lapsed`: reads stop and the snapshot serves; writes wait as `.waitingForAuth` (never `blocked`); Settings shows one quiet row in How You Arrive's style; the token screen is reachable from there. 404 on a table/field → log, treat as empty. 422 → per W0 15.2. Proof rows (`Name` starts `BUILD PROOF`) are dropped in the decoder, in one place.

#### 3.11.3 The snapshot and the CommentIndex
- `Caches/feed-snapshot.v1.json`: records by id, keyed by field id, `cursor` (ISO-8601), `schema: 1`; written atomically with `.completeUntilFirstUserAuthentication`, excluded from backup.
- **Cold launch:** decode off-main (≤ 150 ms), build `CommentIndex`, `RhymeIndex`, `KathaMarks` and the catalog pools off-main, publish once. The Door, the river and every story page draw from it.
- **Refresh, one request in the steady state:** `filterByFormula = IS_AFTER(LAST_MODIFIED_TIME(), '<cursor − 120 s>')` with **no Type and no Status filter**, `returnFieldsByFieldId=true`; merge by id; Status is applied on the client (Live ∪ Staged visible; Archived/Draft removed), so a record that leaves Live leaves the screen.
- **First install, no snapshot:** priority lanes — P0 foundation (Rooms, Archetypes, Threshold Sentences, Field Sounds, Practice, Gaia; filtered by Type; the Door draws from these) · P1 Stories · P2 the full-table pass behind the Door.
- **`loadComments(for:)` becomes a dictionary lookup**; `loadStoryStats` derives from the index; RootView never fetches. App Activity: the same delta by `CREATED_TIME()` into a local log. Codex's earliest day: `maxRecords=1`, `sort Date asc`, `fields[]=Date`, once, cached forever. Small catalogs (Bindu Field, Mandala, Map, Walks): a 24 h freshness check, same pattern.
- **Calibrate the delta once:** edit one story → one record returns; an inverse link changes → confirm the partner's modified time moves, else add it by symmetry; an Archived flip → the record leaves the screen.

#### 3.11.4 The write path
Each `WriteQueue` entry: `{id, clientWriteId, dependsOn, table, method, recordRef, fields, kind, createdAt, attempts, state, lastStatus, lastBody}` in `Application Support/write-queue.json` (atomic, file-protected). **Dependencies:** a child carries `"$ref:<entryId>"` in place of a record id; flush resolves it from the parent's `.landed(recordId)`; a child never sends first (Return → Return Answer, Kept → note PATCH, Letter → Letter Opened, Filament → its activity). **Idempotency:** every POST carries `Client Write Id`; after an ambiguous failure the next attempt first GETs by `{Client Write Id}='<uuid>'`; until the field exists App Activity uses `Notes` `cid:<uuid>`. **States:** `queued · sending · landed · blocked (4xx not auth) · waitingForAuth · sandboxed`; `blocked` retries on launch and on auth recovery. FIFO per dependency chain. `writes.log` rotates at 1 MB (two files); Release lines carry time · kind · method · table · status · record id only.

#### 3.11.5 The ledger, in W0
`Data/LedgerLog` (`ledger.jsonl`, append-only; debounced flush 0.5 s and on background; compacted above 2 MB by W6's fold rule) + `WalkModel` projections, with W6's API names (`note`, `has`, `weight`, `ids`, `last`, `dwellByRoom`). Everything that grows with use is a ledger kind, not a key: `room.memory`, `receivedOn`, `circuit.*`, `carried.ever`, `book.sentBack`, `dot.found`, `plays.met`, `second.offered`, `met.last`, `relay.baton`, `draw.log`, `arrival.surfaced`, `swatch.under`, `deep.carried`, `danceword.surfaced`, `voiceTouched`, `roomMark`. Per-day families become one dictionary key each (`[day: value]`, trimmed to 400 days at launch). Dwell is accumulated in memory and written once per visit, on leave. #### 3.11.6 Observation, rendering, the clock
- **R1 Breath is a function, not a publisher:** `Breath.value(at:)`, `phase(at:)` from the launch-anchored origin; readers take `t` from their own `TimelineView` or engine link; `@EnvironmentObject breath` retires at its 10 view types; `OneBreathTests` stays green.
- **R2** Heavy containers (FeedRiver, the story text, the Book's pages) are `Equatable` views fed ids and value snapshots; per-frame chrome lives in its own leaf structs.
- **R3 Covered layers pause:** `RootView` passes `\.isFrontmost`; every `TimelineView` uses `paused: !isFrontmost`; display-link engines stop; the Metal field holds its last frame; sound is unaffected.
- **R4** No state writes inside `draw` (the sky's focus room is set by `UniverseCamera` on focus change, 0.5 s hysteresis).
- **R5 Canvas discipline:** layouts computed on data change and cached as `Path`s by style bucket; cull to the visible rect + 10 %; level of detail by zoom (below 0.5 px a star is a point); no per-element blur or shadow; hit-testing through a spatial grid (sky, Cloth, eagle-eye, Point rings).
- **R6** `RiverWeft` draws only between visible cards, one overlay `Canvas`, a preference de-duplicated by id and frame.
- **R7 `@Observable` for every container;** never `ObservableObject`, never a `@Published` class (a nested class does not propagate — W5 §2.1, W6 §1, W7 §2.3 all had that shape). `FeedStore` migrates in W0 (`@Environment(FeedStore.self)` at 45 sites).
- **R8** Derived rebuilds (HouseLight, HouseConditions, RhymeIndex) coalesce to once per run-loop turn, off-main where O(n + edges).
- **R9** One haptic engine (§3.10).
- **HouseClock:** every timed behaviour reads `HouseClock.now()` (Release: `Date()`; DEBUG: the harness may override). `Harness.now()` disappears; there is no `#if DEBUG` inside feature logic.

#### 3.11.8 The engineering rules every wave obeys (paste into each score's "How to work")
- **E1** No new stored property on `FeedStore`; new state lives in its own `@Observable` model under `AppModel`; FeedStore may only forward.
- **E2** No new `ObservableObject`; no `@Published` holding a class.
- **E3** Every new persistent value is a `DeviceKey` case (small, bounded) or a ledger kind (anything that grows), with its STATE.md row in the same commit. No per-day key without a prune rule.
- **E4** Every new read goes through `AirtableClient` and the snapshot. No view triggers a network fetch for content the snapshot holds; a new pool is a `Type` filtered in memory.
- **E5** Every new write goes through `AirtableWriter`, declares its dependencies, and has a queue unit test.
- **E6** Every rule, measure, index or layout lives in `Domain/` and is tested there; a test asserts the relationship the design draws, never the port's arithmetic.
- **E7** Per-frame code lives in a leaf, pauses when `!isFrontmost`, and never writes state inside `draw`.
- **E8** Every timed behaviour reads `HouseClock`; the harness overrides only seams.
- **E9** Every static registry gets `.serialized` tests at birth.
- **E10** Every checker or detector ships with bad, good and absent fixtures, run before it is trusted.
- **E11** Routes and arrivals carry record ids; views resolve them from `ContentModel` at render; an unknown id renders absence.
- **E12** Each wave's Done means includes the §3.11.7 budget rows its surfaces touch, measured, with the number in STATUS.md.
- **And from this pass:** every colour, ground, type size, duration and curve comes from `Theme/`; every APP-OWN, cue and VoiceOver string from `HouseWords`; every new hold names its meaning family (§3.6); every new stillness twin names its kind and its breath (§3.7); every new gesture names its lean (§3.8).
