## 1. The ontology as a game board

### 1.1 The board has four axes

The two skills hold a full coordinate system. The Feed can stand on it without showing any of it.

| Axis | What it is | Source | Feed equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WHO speaks** (the body) | The Self experiencing itself through a configuration | the-field SKILL §"Archetypes exist because specific configurations embodied them"; Wazoodle Story Book: "each fabric = a life" (seed-book-format C-1283) | **Suit:** memo, cloth, company, land, machine, lineage, maker, customer… |

A story is one point on this board. A **reveal** happens when the same point has been seen from enough vantages that its opening line changes meaning. Sakshi's *reversible architecture* puts it this way: "The beginning reads differently after the end. Same words. Two recognitions."

### 1.2 Bindu is love in motion, and the player-piece is already defined

`archetypes/bindu.md` describes Bindu in three states. Each one is already a Feed behaviour.

- **MOVING.** "Every interactive element the person touches is the Bindu… When no interaction is needed, the Bindu wanders… it is not asking to be pressed."
  - Feed: the one red point that is every touch target. When nothing is asked, it drifts and cannot be tapped.
- **RESTING.** The goodnight between panels: "says 'I Love You' in the chakra's color, and leaves… By Panel 7, they feel affection for it."
  - Feed: after each received story, the dot rests and writes *I love you* in the room's colour. It never explains itself.
- **SPLITTING.** "Becomes two, three, or infinitely many… never actually divided. The split was velocity."
  - Feed: this is how a story fans out into its rhymes across suits. One point opens into the cloth's telling, the land's and the machine's, then closes back into one dot.
  - This is the *visual* form of "rhyme shown, never a key".

The installation already obeys the house law: "6px red #C0392B, fixed top:16px right:16px, opacity 0.3… Same position in every panel. **Never explained.** The person who notices it was in all 9 panels has the Sakshi moment" (Discovery 98).

The Feed should hold one such dot, in the same place on every surface. That is the architecture staying invisible while still being present.

## 5. EMERGENT: my own concepts

Fourteen ideas the sources spark for the Feed as a game, each with how it would feel to play.

1. **The Board is a Shree Yantra.**
   - The Feed's map is nine enclosures folding inward to a dot. The rooms take their avaranas: Body is the outer square, Garden the 16 petals, Forge the 8 petals, Circle the 14 triangles, Signal the outer 10, Watcher the inner 10, Return the 8 triangles, Remembering the inner triangle, Maya the dot. The structural rooms (Descent, Forgetting, Thread, Field) sit in the four gates of the outer square.
   - Stories glow as points inside their enclosure. Received ones settle inward a hair's width.
   - *Feels like* drifting toward a centre you never reach, because you are already standing on it. No labels anywhere: just a mandala whose lights you recognise.
2. **Turning the Lens.**
   - A received story can be turned like a coin to Gaia, Sid, Arch, Sakshi or Karishma. Each face gives exactly one sentence in that vantage's grammar:
     - Gaia: an asking
     - Sid: a negation ("it did not…")
     - Arch: an open door
     - Sakshi: the seed line
     - Karishma: "it was ready before…", and only after the others
   - When enough faces have been seen, the story's **first line re-renders in gold**, the same words read anew (Sakshi's reversible architecture). Nothing counts how many.
   - *Feels like* the story knowing you have walked all the way round it.
3. **Verbs and their inverses.**
   - Each room receives with its Tree verb and *releases* with the inverse:
     - Body: press, then lift
     - Garden: follow, then stop chasing
     - Circle: open, then pinch the line back into a dot
     - Signal: speak, then sit in its silence
     - Return: receive, then swipe it up like a lantern
   - Releasing sends the story to the Archive book. It is not lost, just held.
   - *Feels like* every story having a hello and a goodbye made with the hands.
4. **The Bindu Memory Sky.**
   - Every word he offers in any reception becomes a ghost star in the Universe register's night sky. Repeated words brighten into constellations.
   - The echo word surfaces in its own time: *"You keep returning to 'home.'"* Never a number.
   - *Feels like* looking up and finding your vocabulary has become weather.
5. **One layer back.**
   - Returning to a story he received weeks ago shows, dimmed beside the new word, the word he gave it last time. Only one layer, never a history.
   - *"The words changed. What they called to you didn't."*
   - *Feels like* meeting yourself on the stairs.
6. **The Oscillation page (a third side of A Maya Game).**
   - The two-sided page gains a third mode: the dot and the text **cannot coexist**. When he reads, the dot vanishes; when he rests, the text vanishes and the dot returns.
   - *Feels like* watching your own attention flicker between being and thinking, and smiling at being caught.
7. **Staying past "done."**
   - After any story's close, a quiet stretch. If he stays about 30 seconds with no prompt, the dot at top-right *wakes*: it grows, warms, and offers the story's **dance**. That is a Bindu performance in the room's element (earth descends, fire burns to a last coal, ether ends in a 5-second echo).
   - Leaving early loses nothing; staying finds more.
   - *Feels like* the curtain call that only the people who stayed in their seats see.
8. **Cross-suit transformation lines.**
   - When two stories from different suits are received in one sitting, the second ends with a line binding them: *"The cloth held 'patience.' The land carried it into 'October.'"*
   - Rhyme *shown* as grammar between beings, not as a key.
   - *Feels like* the beings of the house passing your word hand to hand.
9. **A story's life cycle in the creative-act registers.**
   - The field names eight registers of a made thing: blank page, first mark, unfinished work, editing return, reveal to another, release, return, becoming someone else's.
   - Each story visibly ages through them as paper, not status:
     - the memo is a raw voice mark
     - the story arrives as an unfinished leaf
     - it layers on return (dwell)
     - it is shown to a vantage
     - it is released
     - it ends as **someone else's**: handed as a baton to another being's katha in its rhyme
   - *Feels like* a garden of pages, each at its own age.
10. **The Waiting Water** (from §4).
    - A faintly lit, empty room that is clearly expecting. Water, the missing element, has not surfaced in the body map.
    - *Feels like* entering a room where someone has just set a glass on the table for you.
11. **The Dissolution Walk.**
    - When many received stories rest in the hands at once (never on a schedule, never on a low day), the Feed may offer the Mirror sequence.
    - Each room's latest story is reflected in one line, then undone with its inverse gesture. It ends with *"Everything you placed in this space is already moving toward you."* and "Go play" in root-red.
    - Every station has a stillness path.
    - *Feels like* emptying your pockets at the end of a long, good day.
12. **The breath that leaves.**
    - In each room the reception copy names its exhale: release, forge, give, clear, see. As the rooms rise, the guiding text thins. By the Return and the Remembering, the screen breathes him with no words at all.
    - *Feels like* a teacher stepping back, one room at a time, until you notice you are alone and fine.
13. **The ninth sound.**
    - Each room arrives with its own struck bowl (earth drum through to the trinity), and the Maya room arrives with none. Under the whole app runs an Om at 136.1 Hz, imperceptible, which he only hears in the Remembering's pauses.
    - *Feels like* realising the house has been humming the whole time.
