## 1 · Screening applied

Everything below was screened against the house laws:

- **Private names become roles.** The father/engineer, the mother/the voice at the desk, the elder child/grace, the younger child/shelter, a quilter, a school's wellness keeper, and so on. Archetype names (Sid, Arch, Ashrey, Karishma, Neev and the rest) appear only as *gathering voices*, never inside the story text.
- **Third-party brand and company names are dropped** (the predecessor's factory, the marketplace, the fallen chain store, the company that took the knowledge overseas). Rulings in the record ("teach, never attack", the Silence Architecture) say the same.
- **No business figures.** No revenue, prices, capacities, order counts, square feet, debt or the M&A record (CEN-06 is private by ruling). Where a source leans on a number, the seed turns it into an image.
- **No family conflict.** The SKILL's hold list (C-1106/1107/1108/1131/1142/1179/1357) stays out. The 2009 refusal and the 1988–91 breadwinner years are told as *devotion* and *weather*, not argument.
- **No health details.** The kidney-transplant maker is told only as "carrying a stranger's gift". The pandemic is told as a world-weather and a rhythm, never as illness.
- **Internal-shaped items** (the desk personas, the four countries' reasoning) carry a ⚑ and render only on Ashrey's call. The Feed is his private instrument, so the call is his.

## 2 · The two source architectures in one page

### 2a · The storylines index: an index of what *can* be told

- **232 seeds** (now 364 after the dump) in **8 types**: Company, Team Member, Journey, Product, Moment, **Silence** (21 — what was refused or never made), Community, Customer.
- **Truth Source ladder:** Founder Record → Oral History → Customer Words, with "Both" as a fourth value. A seed *graduates* when the family speaks it or a source surfaces, and the move is "always justified inline".
- **Seven Moves:** match before you make · **tellings append, dated and sourced** (`TELLING RECEIVED (date, source)`) · **never delete, supersede on the record** ("SUPERSEDED WORDING, recorded rather than deleted") · truth moves with a stated reason · conflicts are ruled at the table · identity before crafting · cross-link, don't duplicate.
- **Meta-rulings that read like game physics:** M1 *the table's word is proof* · M2 *ruled once = ruled* · M3 *precision proportional to purpose* · M6 *the five-year rhythm* · and the frame ruling *"Everything lists; nothing is restricted"*. The last one is the record's own version of "conditions light things up and never lock".
- **Codex-Voice-Notes species (Wave 2):** inside-view memos attach *to* seeds and never become seeds — "the story holds what happened; these hold what it felt like from inside." This is exactly the Feed's position relative to the record.

### 2b · The Living Timeline: dual Story + Mirror

- **24 beats** (Beat 0 → 13 plus decimals). Each beat has: Factual Ground · Archetype at Play · **Story Side** (third person, the company) · **Mirror Side** (second person, "You've been here") · **Convergence** (one line true from both sides, the "Sakshi pass") · Benefit Translation.

## 3 · How rhyme is shown (no gates): a small display vocabulary used below

| Code | Display | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **R-FP** | **Facing page.** The two stories open as a spread, a left and a right page. Two-Door/bilateral pairs. | The same moment seen from two bodies at once |
| **R-MT** | **Margin thread.** A fine coloured thread in the river runs from one card to the other (the Timeline's thread colours). | You see the connection without being sent anywhere |
| **R-WG** | **Word glow.** A word both stories share warms in both while one is open (e.g. *red*, *ground*, *name*). | The language itself rhymes |
| **R-EL** | **Echo line.** The last line of one story sits faint above the first line of the other (the Creator §02→§07 echo technique). | One telling hands off to the next |
| **R-SD** | **Same day.** Both stories surface on the same calendar day or year-anniversary (e.g. 3 March, 13 April). | Time rhymes |
| **R-ST** | **Strata.** The rhyming story shows as a lower layer under this one, visible by dwell. | Depth, not sequence |

## 6 · The Timeline's dual Story + Mirror architecture as game mechanics

1. **The turn (Story → Mirror → Convergence).** Every self-katha can carry three faces:
   - the being's telling (Story, third or first person);
   - a short **Mirror** in second person ("You have carried something that was never yours");
   - the **Convergence**, one line true from both sides.

   *Play:* after the story, the page **turns by itself on a still breath**, or by a swipe for touch. The Mirror addresses Ashrey directly, and it says *you*, never his name. The Convergence line rises only when **both faces have been dwelt on**. Dwell lights it; nothing locks it. Convergence lines are the Archive book's margin: a book that slowly fills with lines that are true from both sides.

2. **The Two-Door Principle → two bodies, one moment.** Every FULL BILATERAL moment (acquisition; Phoenix; first lamination; pandemic) gets **two self-kathas by two beings**: the forge's being and the threshold's being. For example: C-11 the truck / C-12 the inbox; C-01 the No / C-02 the ground. Whichever door he enters, the other door shows as a lit card across the river (R-FP). LIGHT ECHO moments show only an R-EL line. The four treatment classes in `bilateral-echo-map.md` set **how loud the rhyme display is**.

3. **Nine spines as story-signatures.** Tag each self-katha (invisibly) with its spine. The spine chooses its river placement and its closing gesture:
   - Accumulation stories add a line on each return by dwell;
   - Rhyme stories open as spreads;
   - Activation stories start dark and light all at once;
   - Inversion stories begin upside-down;
   - Cartography stories open as a map you pan;
   - Recognition stories bring the outside voice in last;
   - Return stories echo their own opening;
   - Synthesis stories braid several threads;
   - Threshold stories end as a door.

   This gives a library of 9 closings to pair with the 12 signatures already built.

4. **Silences held → "what this story doesn't say".** Every Timeline section lists its silences. *Play:* a long dwell on the white space after the last line reveals one faint sentence: what the story chose **not** to tell, and why. The Fabrite name, the figures and the conflict never appear. Only the fact that something was held appears, as respect.

5. **Weave test / Mirror test → authoring QA for every new self-katha.** These are invisible to Ashrey and part of the build checklist. Remove the concrete and it goes thin; remove the meaning and it goes dry. Does he feel it in his own structure?

6. **Thread filters → voice threads.** The Timeline's coloured threads (steel blue, auburn, forest green, and soft gold proposed for grace) become the colours of the margin threads in the river. Following a colour is how he walks one lineage through all suits. That is a *walk*, not a filter that hides.

7. **"Now yours" → the Present.** The Timeline ends at Beat 13 ("Ashrey → Sakshi → You"). In the Feed, the river's last stone is **today's Codex memo**, placed beside the beat it rhymes with (see EMERGENT #2).

## 7 · The seed → telling graduation as game mechanics

The storylines' law maps almost one-to-one onto a living story:

| Record law | Feed mechanic | Feel |

1. A new Codex memo or video transcript arrives.

3. If it matches, a "telling arrived" stratum appears on that seed.
4. If nothing holds it, a new seed is born in the river.
