## 1 · The library: forms the Feed could become

Tags: **(a)** a new ROOM · **(b)** a way to RECEIVE a story · **(c)** a GAME MOVE · **(d)** how an other-suit story could LOOK. Some elements in the corpus carried a lock, a one-a-day limit or a count. Where an entry here comes from one of those, it says how to take it "lit, not locked".

### (a) New rooms: places with one mechanism (S13), each could hold a register or a suit
1. **The Alignment.** Three rooms drawn out of register in red, green and blue slide into one white room only while the reader is still. A room for stories about integration. (EC · Rooms that each do one thing, 6.2 Mandala)
2. **The Closing Room.** The walls, ceiling and floor close in while the reader stands still, and never reach them. For stories about pressure that turned out to be holding. (EC · Rooms that each do one thing)
3. **The Pool.** Only what a travelling pool of light rests on exists. A room for attention, so the Watcher's sibling. (EC · Rooms that each do one thing)
4. **No Far Wall.** Thresholds arrive out of the fog faster than they can be crossed. A room for the Forgetting's suit, or for futures. (EC · Rooms that each do one thing)
5. **The Membrane House.** No entrance; membrane walls lit from behind move with the breath. For the Body. (EC · Rooms that each do one thing)
6. **The Climb.** A vertical room where each altitude has its own light physics: topaz raking sun, sapphire ring waves, the fogged pearl that has no light source, the cat's-eye band. Tempo slows going up, and a rail names the region. A natural room for the lineage or the Land suit. (EC · Weather by altitude; SH S12)
7. **The Mirror Walk.** A room under the floor, entered by a hold that resists for about 1.25 s, followed by:
   - a tunnel of three teachings, word by word;
   - LOOK, with eyes for the O's;
   - naming, the Glass, an out-of-order inversion, and the carve;

   Any katha could be authored as a "walk score". (SH S15; EC · The tunnel, LOOK, A walk as pure data)
8. **The Portrait Sky.** Every story in the Feed is a star on the golden angle, lit in five tiers (here · open · ground · ahead · veiled) under the real hour and season. A day reads as a glyph, a month as a letter, a year as a ring. (SH S16; EC · The sky of every page, A day as one glyph, A month as a letter) (PortraitView exists; the month-letter and year-ring look new)
9. **The Instrument Room of the Suits.** The suits sit as stations around the Point: cloth, land, machine, lineage, makers, the returning customer, cosmos.
   - One travelling dot rests at each station.
   - Touch a station and it says what it *takes in · gives out · keeps*.

   (SH S17; EC · The station ring and its travelling dot)
10. **The World in a Box.** A small world under the real sky and season (petals, fireflies, leaves, snow). Its objects are grown from what was received elsewhere in the Feed, and it leaves one small gift a day (pebble, feather, key…) to place by hand. (EC · Under the real hour and season)
11. **The River of Letters around NOW.** Kathas sealed forward sit above a NOW line; kathas addressed back to an age, a year or a feeling sit below it. Lit, not locked: a forward letter glows "opens in its season" and can still be read plainly. (EC · The river of letters; Things that grow by real time) (a river surface exists in PresentView, so verify the overlap)
12. **The Seven Gates (Release).** A descent of arches in three states (lit like a torch / passed / waiting). At each gate the reader lays something down with a 2.6 s hold, and gifts rise on the way back. (EC · The arch in three states, Laying something down)

### (b) Ways to receive a story
21. **Word by word, breathing at punctuation.** About 250 ms a word, +430 ms after a full stop, each word sharpening out of a 4 px blur. (EC 6.1)
22. **Focus, not fade.** Lines rise while their blur clears, on a fixed clock (0.6 / 5 / 10.5 / 17 s); the text layer breathes over 9 s. (EC 6.1)
23. **Clause by clause.** One clause at a time at breath pace, then a faint source line ("from C-1270, Feb") in lower ink; a tap replays it. (EC 6.1)
24. **Words sharpen as you reach them.** The story's last line stays blurred until the reader scrolls to it, earned by everything above it. (EC 6.1)
25. **A phrase that assembles out of order.** One inversion per katha: the words land in a fixed permutation, then settle. (EC 6.1)
26. **Letter-spacing closing.** A recognition line whose letters draw together (.2em → .04em, about 2 s). (EC 6.1)
27. **Each line one shade lighter.** A three-line witness whose ink rises step by step toward the light. (EC 6.1)
28. **A letter revealed phrase by phrase in a sealed envelope.** The seal pulses while the letter is "being written"; each phrase is held for `min(900, 180+16·len)` ms; it ends *keep in the book · keep unread*. (EC 6.1)
29. **The paper sheet that rises.** Whatever is received arrives on paper rising over the playing-cloth ground (1100 ms). (EC 6.1)
30. **Five ways for a paragraph to arrive:**
    - surfacing out of white (stillness);
    - along a ray (following);
    - through a parting veil that stays thinner where it was read;
    - letterpress, deeper the harder the press;
    - landing in flight;
    - also mirror panes, and a star sent out that has to return.

    (EC · 6.2 More chassis-bound)
31. **Hold to read, with a cadence per speaker.** The heart phrase arrives slower and in ember; on release, the last word becomes an ember that brightens with time away. (EC · Learning engines) (ReadByHandView in app)
32. **Where you stayed.** After a held reading, the 2–3 phrases the reader dwelt on are handed back, and only then is the voice named. (EC · Learning engines)
33. **One essay told twice around a threshold.** The katha is read inward, a threshold ("the same movement · seen from both ends"), then the same katha comes back outward, answering itself pair by pair. (EC · Bindu Blink)
34. **A story in panels with a closer look.** Full-bleed panels with three held beats (kindle · part · spread), then pinch into one dense image to find hidden points. (EC · Learning engines)
35. **The sealed letter for one person.** A dedication, then a seed, then begin, advancing every 3 s. For kathas addressed to the reader. (EC · Field)
36. **The staged answer.** A drifting fog takes on the colour of the story that answers. The verb comes at 0.4 s, the name at 2.2 s, the reason at 3.4 s. (EC · Field)
37. **The verb arriving.** One word alone at 64–84 pt light italic inside its aura, as a katha's single-word threshold. (EC · Field)
38. **The layered player.** The story keeps performing while its reading rises over it as a sheet at 80 %; chrome is absent except a ghost verb. (EC · Field)
39. **Breath as navigation.** The story's lines come nearer on the inhale and recede on the exhale; drag is the equal path. (EC · Field)
40. **A record walked as a shaft.** A story's fields wait as stations in a tunnel of its own outline:

## 2 · Checklist for designers: laws and measured fixes a new room or grammar must respect

**The floors**
- **Truth:** no invented count and no claimed past.
- **Access:** a keyboard path, a way out, and contrast for text.
- **The experience as designed is the law** (3 Oct): a new rule may add to an existing room, never subtract a designed hold, wait, gate, count, sound or line. (SKILL.md)

**Holds**
- A hold completes only by a real hold, or by Enter/Space held for the same length (key repeat ignored).
- An early release drains with nothing done.
- What the hold opens waits for the release.
- A tap completes a hold only where the design gave it one. (SKILL.md law 16)
- **Measured defect to avoid:** `rk.js` `.hold` calls `done()` on Enter keydown (instant), and the Hold row in `elements.md` still says "keyboard = instant". Both predate law 16. New grammars must follow law 16, not the core runtime.
- Reduced motion stills the drawing, never the hold, and keeps the order of events (EC · Reduced motion keeps the order). A hold bar may measure only the live gesture and store nothing.

**Chrome and contrast**
- Interfaces thin with depth: chrome at 100 → 80 → 60 → 25 → 0 %.
- **Text in the chrome is full ink or hidden; half ink fails contrast.**
- A touch, hover or focus brings a control back for a few seconds.
- "Surface" or Esc is always reachable. (law 6; EC · The interface thins with depth)
- Measured on the Feed's grounds (feed.css):
  - ink at 35 % = 2.81:1, decorative only;
  - ink-2 `#94908F` = 6.15:1;
  - Dawn labels at 66 % on `#0B0910` measured 4.12, lifted to `#8F735C` = 4.50;
  - eyebrow on wash `#9B7155` = 4.62;
  - in the Light: living ink 12.22, ink-2 `#545051` 5.28; settled/carved ink `#A79D8E` is 1.78 and must never carry reading text.
- **Contrast fixes keep the hue:** move only OKLCH lightness and re-measure (site-v3: 472 colours fixed this way).
- Contrast is measured *at rest, after resting filters*. The Turning's saturate .32 / brightness .70 must still clear 4.5:1. (verification Gate 4; EC · Colour through staying)
- Each of the eight day colours, set as body text on its tinted card, must clear 4.5:1; also check ink on the brightest compose wash. On void grounds use APCA Lc 75 for body text.
- Mono text never below 11 px. Honesty badges designed at 7 px render at 11 px minimum with an accessible name.
- A control that rests faded is unusable by the person who needs it: rest it at its hover value. Never lock zoom. (site-v3)

**Counts**
- **Numbers describe the subject, never the reader.** The detector to run: `\d+ (of|/) \d+` about the reader, "%" next to a person, "streak", "days since", "behind", "awakened".
- An invented count never shows: "84 hearts", "0/7 awakened" and "2/9" were all removed. (law 15; EC §7)

**Light, never locks**
- Status shows in five tiers of light, with "ahead" behind a dashed, uncrossed line.
- Dim marks still clear 3:1 and are named in text ("ahead, not yet walked"). (law 14)
- Corpus items that carried a lock or a one-a-day limit must be re-cast as light before entering the Feed under Ashrey's 12 Oct ruling:
  - locked rooms (CV2);
  - the learning app's 33 h pacing gate;
  - "one message a day … quiet until tomorrow";
  - the vow's "one redraw a day";
  - the gentle door that "never repeats in the period";
  - forward letters "sealed until their date".

**Absence and empty states**
- Missing is absent: a way that does not exist isn't drawn, and the ways that exist weigh the same. (law 22)
- The quiet state is designed first, with no apology: "The field is quiet here." (law 19)

**Memory**
- One store on the device, one prefix, one key per thing.
- *forget me* clears everything; *carry your road* exports it.
- No reader state ever goes in a URL; review states are forced only from the bench or harness.
- Walk continuity uses one sessionStorage object with a 90-minute TTL, and a ceremony writes nothing back ("not a place that keeps score").
- Safari evicts storage after seven days without a visit; every memory element needs a keep-it path. (laws 26; EC · Walk continuity; research §5)

**Ceremony grammar**
- Open with one breath, and close with a hold that carves (takes on) or lays down (lets go).
- The world answers in its own material.
- The close is a whisper and one warmer light, never a checkmark: "The room has changed."
- **Never fabricate a voice's response to show that something happened.** (SH S14; EC · Released)
- Who speaks in full is drawn from the day, never from how many times the reader has returned; one voice is never silent. (ruled 11 Oct)

**Crossings and motion**
- **Every crossing passes through something** (dissolve 0.28 s · Veil · Descent 2.35 s · passage 5.4 s), never a slide or a hard swap.
- The blink means one crossing only.
- The colour flood stays under .92 opacity and never repeats within one second.
- Flashes: steps of at least 250 ms, under three per second.
- Beat rings: at most one per 1.08 s.
- 1 Hz pulses stay soft (opacity, not brightness jumps). (law 4; EC 6.4)
- Every inward pass has its outward pass, designed separately rather than as the first one reversed. (law 3)
- Type changes only where a crossing means something. (law 9)
- Every place moves as what it is: the Feed has 13 motion signatures, and the Forgetting's jitter is 2 px or less. Tempo is open. (laws 8, 7; EC · Thirteen motion signatures)
- Swell by `scale`, never by a property that reflows the page; animate letter-spacing only on a line that sits alone. Arrive by class, never by frame. (site-v3)
- Overlap is a real defect class: screenshot and read at seven sizes (357 collisions were found and fixed).

**Sound**
- New sound takes the two roots: 110 Hz for the rooms about the reader, 136.1 Hz for the worlds and the point. Existing sound keeps its pitch. In an app, sound starts on the first touch. Binaural is offered, never required. (law 10)
- Sound answers the hand: strain, give, coming into tune, a tone held while writing, a swell with stillness. (law 11)

**Gestures and stillness**
- Stillness is an input: it accrues with the hand off and no key pressed; a touch pauses it and never resets it. (law 18)
- Readiness is sensed; crossing is chosen. (law 17)
- Every gesture has an equal path: drag beside tilt, a list of regions beside the body figure, a list view beside the sphere.
- Two taps, never one, on dense maps.

**Figures**
- Figures are pure functions of (t, kind, hue, count, progress), with one seeded random and one visibility-gated loop. (law 12)
- **The room test:** could this form belong to any other subject? If so, it is wrong. Mechanism, never mood. (law 13)

**Generated text and wording**
- Generated text is labelled.
- Persona text is never presented as the person's own words, and the person's past voice is never generated.
- A live seam carries written laws plus an authored fallback. (EC · The live seam; One voice record)
- Wording canon is load-bearing ("What arrived for you?" · "The room has changed." · "The field gathers"). Every line has a single source. (EC 6.4)
- Story text is in the DOM, or accessible, from the start; a reveal never hides it from screen readers.

**Guards that cross into stories**
- No seed syllables in any script.
- The Mandala's beings are named, never drawn.
- The camera is never on by default.
- No person appears on a shared surface without consent, and people appear only as colours: a hue from a name around the golden angle, at one lightness.
- Family, health and business-figure content stays out (EC §7, matching the house laws).

**Method**
- Score before build: five tracks (scene · sound · gesture · motion · logic) with timings.
- Run the parity pass and name each defect as phase drift, range compression or detail erosion. Give each screen 3–5 feel anchors; "4 of 5 is a fail". (laws 23–24; EC · The parity pass)
- **Every opening calls a closing** (CV2 pair-calls):
  - a stitch must return;
  - a hold at the door wants a release at the exit;
  - a seed wants its tense-shifted return;
  - a stat wants its source;
  - a voice wants real permission.
