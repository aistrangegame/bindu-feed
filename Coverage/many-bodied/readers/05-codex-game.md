## 4. Explicit tensions with the house laws, and how his own words resolve them

1. **Counting.** In Mar 2025 he designed points, levels and streaks (C-1068 and C-1069; the quest tiers in C-1101). From Sep 2025 on, he moves the other way:
   - Levels become the universe's (C-1152) and an illusion (C-1181).
   - "level games... keep you occupied here" (C-1230).
   - "Qualitatively... Not like, do this, do that... Just like, where are you?" (C-1232).
   - Gains "don't need to be part of the system" (C-1228).
   - The bliss isn't chased (C-1169), and calling something special "collapses" it (C-1284).
   - **Resolution:** keep the shape of levels, quests and journeys, but never show a number, a rank or a streak. The ruling is right, and his later voice supports it.
2. **Notifications.** He asked for them three times. C-1353 wants random-time gifts of a story, energy and song. C-1088 wants a daily intention, story, planner and performance. C-1057 wants a phone message when he is low.
   - **Resolution:** keep what he actually wanted, which is the not-knowing and the gift. Move it inside the app. A gift has already arrived at an unpredictable hour and waits to be found. Nothing reaches outward.
3. **Gating.** C-1300 plans a "gated experience" that asks for a login. That idea was for the business storybook, not the Feed. C-1280 has the last page opened by an intention or ask for help. C-1279 says the reader may "jump straight to the end".
   - **Resolution:** the ask is an invitation that lights things up, not a key. Both paths stay open, which matches the 12 Oct ruling.
4. **Depth by visit count.** C-1354 describes depth by return visits: "The second time I come back and read. Third time". The house law says layers appear by dwell, never by visit count.
   - **Resolution:** the law stands. His intent, deeper on return, is met by dwell-on-return, a signature already built.
5. **Low days.** C-1057 wanted the Codex to reach him "when I'm in an unconscious state". C-1154 and C-1151 say suffering must be allowed to be felt and not be interrupted: "allow the suffering to happen and allow it to truly happen."
   - **Resolution:** on a low day the game does not push. Only stillness is offered. This fits "never on a low day".
6. **Announcing rewards.** Telling about a high state drains it (C-1047, C-1195), and "The mark is in the architecture, not in the claiming of it" (C-1338).
   - **Resolution:** no achievement toasts or "you unlocked" lines. The architecture stays invisible.
7. **Builder versus experiencer.** His builder mode repeatedly takes over his life (C-1090, C-1091, C-1116, C-1236 "I build an elaborate system to prevent failure, the system becomes overwhelming"). C-1111 blesses play mode.
   - **Resolution:** this supports the ruling that he is the experiencer. The game should never hand him maintenance chores.
8. **Giving something up versus getting.** C-1230: "it has to be something to get, not something to give up". He rejected his own 30-day challenge model (C-1232).
   - **Resolution:** no penalties, no losses and no "don't break the chain".
