### Chakras without a room (candidates for new rooms or receptions)

1. **Vishuddha + Sonar + Echo: the throat as one instrument, sending and receiving.** There is no "said" room. Stories of the held sentence, the precise word and the long listen are scattered across Signal and Circle. Candidate room: **The Said** (or **The Listening**). Its reception grammar is *nothing appears until the story has been allowed to finish*. Echo's energy peaks at the end, so the room's layers arrive only after the last line.
2. **Axis: the blink.** It is the hinge of every room, not a room. It is better as a **reception grammar that lives everywhere**: closing the eyes or covering the screen flips a story to its dark/light other side. See the Axis Flip in §2.
3. **Crow: what remains when narration stops.** Candidate room: **The Summit** or **After the Shaking**, for stories where the body outran the commentary. Its grammar is the only one that addresses the reader directly ("I am asking you to do something now"), matching Crow's single fourth-wall break.
4. **Aatma: the heartwood triangle.** Candidate reception rather than room: **The Trikona**. Any story can be turned into three facets (Will, Knowledge, Action), and the reader sees which one the story was secretly about.
5. **Inspiration and Intuition as "help from up ahead".** Strong Codex anchor: Principle 4, the Reverse-Help Dynamic ("need is prayer reaching backward through time"). Signal and Remembering already hold such stories. A **Future-Kin** reception could gather them.
6. **Heart-Field and the E Chakras.** No room holds "authored registers". Candidate: **The Unnumbered**, a room that grows its own new chakras from Codex entries (Level 2, Lalita Play).
7. **The Interlude.** There is no between-space anywhere in the Feed. Candidate: an **Interlude** reception between any two stories, reached at random. See §5.
