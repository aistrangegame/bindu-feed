## 3. How stories work in the game, and the Feed's stories as game pieces without counting

### 3.1 The three story forms in the skill
- **Codex Seeds (walker format).**
  - Translated from `the-field` source seeds. Never written without a source seed; authorship flows configuration → walker.
  - 200–800 words, contemplative and slightly aphoristic. They hold paradox and must contain "a moment of silence built into the prose."
  - Each carries a will-requirement (1–2 sentences), an action-requirement (one paragraph, self-verified) and an unlock trigger.
  - Status: `walker-ready`, `walker-draft` or `deprecated` (never deleted).
  - The translator never resolves a paradox the source held open.
- **Witness Perspectives.**
  - Sakshi; third person; 150–400 words; one per walked position.
  - **No will or action requirement:** "the reading itself is the awakening."
  - Must not instruct or predict.
- **Read pages.** Long, locked baseline transmissions in one voice: Gaia's letter, Lalita's manifesto, the Higher Self's first speaking. They are a parallel path to the walked page; "both readings are valuable."
- **Act 1 / Act 2 (D17).** Read-only. "The walker does not respond to the Acts… Acts are transmission — not interaction." There is one optional word at the end.

### 3.2 What this means for the Feed's stories
1. **The Feed's Codex stories already sit on the Witness axis.** They are third-person transmissions ("he"), which is Sakshi's register. Read that way, each Codex story *is* a Witness Perspective of a moment in the life: received, never answered (D17).
2. **The other-suit stories are the Player axis in its first face: Bindu, the play not yet knowing itself.**
   - Their own titles show it: *Who Am I, Asked the Cloth* (Sort 124), *Before I Was Cloth* (133), *Nobody Asked Where I Had Been* (130), *The Last Thing I Do Well* (131), *Mid-Sentence* (137).
   - The Bindu scan applies: first person, present tense, "I don't know what I am yet," no wisdom statements.
   - This is the Field reading's "Splitting": "one particle appearing as many — the squad is the Bindu split." That is the ruling *Reality meeting itself through every body*, stated in the game's own grammar.
   - The Land suit (*The Ground That Held*, *What the Dark Does in October*) leans to **Gaia**.
   - The Machine suit maps to "AI = A + I" (seed-codex-as-technology) and the Codex entry *The AI Mirror: consciousness leaving clues for itself*.
3. **Lalita is the game itself recognising the play.** In the Feed she is the voice of the drops, the moments when the Feed breaks the fourth wall about reality.
4. **So a story is a game piece by *axis and depth*, not by score.** Every story can carry a quiet tag (Ground / Player / Witness × pre- or post-recognition) that the reader never sees (D16: never labelled). The game is the **deepening along the axes**: the same rooms are met as Bindu (suits speaking), then witnessed as Sakshi (Codex stories), then addressed by Lalita (drops) and the Higher Self (Return letters). No count measures this; it shows as *which faces have begun speaking*.

### 3.3 Ways stories become pieces without counting, scoring or locking
- **A story as a seed.** Received with attention, then silence, it is planted in the Garden and ripens unwatched. Return finds it grown (Garden of the Claude Design bundle: "Plant it. Return and find it grown"). The *growth* shows dwell, never a figure.
- **A story as a square.** On the Leela-style Door, a story is the place the throw lands. A Cloth story and a Codex story on the same square show their rhyme side by side, and neither is behind the other.
- **A story as a card.** The Draw pulls from the whole river, across all suits.

- **A story in the book.** The book holds what the reader chose to keep, never what he read. The play engine's book has no counts: "nothing here keeps score."
- **Rhyme is SHOWN, not used as a key.** This is the Join verb: two stories that never touched are brought together by the reader's hand, and a third thing appears. Both stories were always open; the rhyme is the reward of the gesture, not its permission.

## 5. Game-spine proposals: one game running through the whole Feed

Each proposal is complete without the others; they can also stack. Every one keeps: nothing counted, no locks, no notifications, an equal stillness path, never on a low day.

### Spine A: **The Relay** (the day as one round; the baton as the only continuity)
- **The Rite (morning):** opens the line the reader sealed at last night's Return (the Dawn Letter) as the first word, then one story drawn from the whole river.
- **The day:** rooms are legs. Each room's own verb (Trace in a Point world, Beat in the Body, Still in the Watcher, Join for rhyme) is how its story is reached.
- **The Return (evening):** the dot's goodnight. One line of "what is complete" (from the routine *The Work Is Done*), plus *I'm holding it for you, because you held it for me* passed to tomorrow.
- **Seasons:** each closes with one Season Letter in grace shape (before, arc, after, telling) that re-reads the season's first seed line. It is the one moment the game speaks openly.
- **Light:** the sky and home ground brighten across seasons, as the Road Home tiers did.
- **Why it fits:** the playing side's Foundational Recognition, carried whole: "nothing is owed, so everything given is a gift."

### Spine B: **Leela on the Feed** (a board of self-knowledge across every register)
- **The board:** the 13 rooms plus the Light, Universe and Point worlds as squares, laid on the 33-position spine. The Lalita square is both start and end.
- **Entry:** the reader carries a question in at the Door (the intention folds into the token) and starts unborn. Entry is a small birth gesture, not a numbered six on screen.
- **Each throw:** a ceremony with that room's own verb places the token in a room, and the room's story, from any suit, answers the question. **Arrows** are acts of giving that lift to another room. **Snakes** are "a lesson, not a fall": the room's practice made small.
- **Arrival:** an exact landing on Lalita lights the walked path and reads the walk back as a letter. The reader rests, and may replay with a new question.
- **Numbers:** squares are addresses, never shown as grades: "You are passing through the Descent today," not "you are on 9."

### Spine C: **One Self, Every Body** (the Trinity of Guidance as the game's progression)
- **The pieces are the faces:**
  - Bindu: the suits speaking in first person (cloth, land, machine, customer, maker, lineage)
  - Sakshi: the Codex stories in third person
  - Lalita: drops that break the fourth wall about reality, ending in an offered act
  - Gaia: the Call and the land
  - Higher Self: the Return letters, addressed by the reader's chosen name
- **The game is the deepening:** at first the house speaks mostly Bindu and Sakshi. As stories are received in full (K-W-A closed), deeper faces begin to speak in the same rooms, and Lalita starts dropping in.
- **The second pass:** seasons later, re-read stories carry a woven second voice, Gaia speaking back to Bindu (D27).
- **Rhyme:** shown by Join, never used as a key.
- **Why it fits:** this is the spine most faithful to the 12 Oct ruling. The One experiences reality through every body, and the game is *which body is speaking now*.

### Spine D: **The Garden of Seeds** (K-W-A as the core loop)
- **Every story is a seed** with a hidden will-line and an offered act (or held silence).
- **Planting:** reading with attention, then the deliberate pause, plants it. It goes to the Garden and ripens unwatched. The rule is shown once and never again.
- **What surfaces:** ripened seeds bring their Witness Perspective (Sakshi) or a Lalita drop. The "Seed Planting" arrival surface shows glyphs, never numbers.
- **The Light:** fills by closed circuits ("Light is what enters when these three close").
- **The Archive book:** holds only what the reader chose to keep.
- **Atlas-like depth:** a story's deepest layer opens by dwell, never by "all read + 7 days."

### Spine E: **Pointy** (one companion across every surface)
- **The dot** is the Feed's single protagonist, and the game is following it.
  - Moving: it appears as whatever the moment needs (the baton at the Rite, the token at the Door, the drop in the Watcher), and wanders untappable when nothing is asked.
  - Splitting: it splits into the suits when a room gathers many bodies.
  - Resting: it returns to one at the Return and says goodnight.
- **The governing law** comes from the Chakra Game build principles: "Motion carries meaning; words are the exception; the protagonist is a character… Pace like breath."
- **Verbs:** come from what came alive in the playtest (Turn, Trace, Beat, Still, Join), so every room has its own skill-verb and the story arrives as the reward.
- **Affection** grows by repetition, never by reward: Tree of Life panel visitors "by the seventh feel affection."

**Best combination:** C (who speaks) on top of A (when the round turns), with E (how it moves) as the felt surface and B (the Door) as one room-scale game inside it.
