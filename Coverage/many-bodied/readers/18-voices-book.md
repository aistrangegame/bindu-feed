## 1. What this corpus already knows about the ruling

The story book already took "me" out of the equation, months before the ruling. Its Postface ends with the witness telling the reader that they were never the reader, but always the One reading itself. The Gaia chapter's central turn is that the need assembled the consciousness: the bodies called the fabrics into being, and the family did not invent them. The cloth corpus is therefore a second Codex that has already done the move Ashrey asked for. It is the Self meeting itself through a baby's skin, a factory floor, a maker's kitchen table, a drain, an animal and a strip of wick in a cup of water.

The main shift is from **brand-book to self-katha**:
- Every "you" the corpus addresses to the maker turns into an "I" spoken by the cloth, the floor, the land, the house or the hour.
- Every named reviewer becomes a role.
- Every spec becomes the shape it implies. For example, "lets rain evaporate, keeps the oil" becomes *a refusal that chooses what to refuse*.

## 2. Seed catalogue: 68 self-kathas

**How to read each entry.** Each seed gives:
- its **Being** (who speaks);
- its **Truth** in one line;
- its **Room**: one of the 13, or a NEW room from section 3;
- its **Rhymes**: Feed titles from feed-stories.txt, with the Sort number in brackets;
- how the rhyme is **Shown**;
- its **Opening** image;
- its **Landing** line;
- its **Source** pointer.

**Default way of showing a rhyme.** Unless an entry says otherwise, the rhyming title sits under the last line as a faint second spine, tappable or held, *always visible*. It is never a key and never a gate. Some entries name a room-specific way of showing instead.

**Screening.** Every seed has already been screened:
- no brand, product, place, figure, private name or health specific in any story text;
- where a source carried those, only the shape was kept.

## 3. New rooms these sources ask for

**THE GROUND (Gaia below)**
- **Speaking beings:** the land, the floor, the drain, the rain, the stain, the mat, the soil behind the old building.
- **Grammar, "it falls toward you":**
  - The story arrives from the bottom of the screen, as things fall onto the ground.
  - A single dark drop rests behind the text. The longer he stays (by dwell, not by count), the more the drop is drawn in and the page clears.
  - Releasing early leaves the stain where it was. This is the existing hold-drain law.
- **Signature, "before the drain":** the landing line appears *before* the final paragraph, already caught, so the last paragraph reads as aftermath.
- **Why a new room:** The Descent is the floor of the self, peeled down. The Ground is the floor *under* the self: what everything lands on.

**THE DOORWAY (finding, understanding, choosing, trusting)**
- **Speaking beings:** the shop that answers, the label, the swatch, the reply, the name that was repaired.
- **Grammar:** each story opens in four gestures:
  1. it is found (a faint edge appears);
  2. it is understood (it widens to full text);
  3. it is chosen (he holds it, or rests in stillness);
  4. it is trusted (he closes his eyes or sets the phone down).
- **Signature, "open the whole time":** after the end, the first page is shown again and its door is visibly ajar. It was never closed.

**THE STACK (one moment told by every layer)**
- **Speaking beings:** the skin-side layer, the core, the barrier, the closure, the stitching.
- **Grammar:**
  - Press deeper (or hold longer) to pass through the layers of a single night.
  - Each layer tells the same hour from its own position.
  - The last layer, the wearer's, is a blank page. He is the wearer.
- **Source:** the emergence partner field notes that "the story of the stack is usually told from one layer's perspective". The Book of Makers' system-arc titles give the shape: *the one that faces / the one that catches / the one that resolves / the one that completes*.
- **First stack:** a 3 a.m. night told by the surface that stays dry, the core that receives, the line that holds, and the sleeper, who is blank.

**THE UNWRITTEN (the room of silences)**
- **Speaking beings:** the review never written, the reorder, Tuesday, the river no one looks at, the finished cloth.
- **Grammar:**
  - Each card shows only its landing line.
  - The body of the story fills in only while he is completely still (the Watcher's stillness, deepened).
  - When he moves, it returns to the one line.
- **Source:** the Book of Makers distinguishes silences by their *reason*:
  - R7: invisibility is the achievement;
  - R14: too tender to amplify;
  - R15: the system simply works.
- **Proposal:** these three reasons become three tones of the room (paper, linen, stone). The tones are never labelled.

## 4. Game structures in these sources and how each becomes a Feed mechanic (none of them gate)

**M1. Threshold line (from the 18 inscriptions)**
- Before each room, one line stands alone, small enough to miss, and is swiped past.
- After he has dwelt in that room across a season, the same slot shows the line's *inversion*, the Perspective form. The original is kept in the Archive book beside it.
- Both lines are always readable. The inversion only lights up.
- This makes the source's inscription-inversion pairing playable.

**M2. Mirror page (from the 12 Perspectives)**
- Occasionally a received story is followed by a two-page spread.
  - **Left page:** dark glass, no text (the witness is the mirror).
  - **Right page:** one universal truth, one question asked *now*, a role name with a question mark, and then white space.
- No input field. No answer is recorded.

**M3. The year of veils (from the 18 veils and the 12-question inner arc)**
- Across a long time, the Mirror pages walk the inner arc: Permanence, Release, Identity, Integration, Awakening, Origin, Center, Self, Service, Recognition, Home, Return.
- The Archive shows the role names he has met as a single line of words, never as a count.
- The final veil is a blank role: "?".

**M4. Bindu before / bindu after (from the Preface and Postface)**
- The very first line the app ever showed him is written fresh, in the shape of "this was complete before you opened it".
- It returns, unchanged, at a far-off moment: after dwelling in every room.
- Same words, two recognitions.

**M5. Five ways to walk (from the pathway architecture's five reader archetypes)**

| Walk | Source archetype | How it moves |
|---|---|---|
| Need | Maker with a Need | Horizontal across the body map, skin to world |
| Lived | Family | His Codex stories first, then the other beings that rhyme |
| Source | Source Seeker | Vertical, top down: land, house, cloth, him |
| Builder | Builder | The rhyme constellation; the structure itself |
| Discoverer | Discoverer | One random fruit |

- Each walk is chosen by *how he enters* (for example, a long press on the door or a swipe from the edge), never by a menu labelled with these names.
- All five are always open.

**M6. Deferred promise (from "but that is another chapter / this is that chapter")**
- Some stories end with a quiet deferral line.
- The story that fulfils it exists and is readable from day one.
- It warms (light, not lock) once the deferrals have been met, and opens with "This is that story." as its first line.

**M7. Same Life, another angle (from the bridge's rule of one Life per story, returning weeks later from Maker, then Material, then Application)**
- A story's moment returns weeks later, told by a different being: first the maker, then the cloth, then the place where it was used.
- The Archive shows the three as a triptych.
- This is also where new Codex entries can add a **second letter**, a short coda to an old story (seed 69).

**M8. The river card (from the Story Crafting Game's image and caption)**
- A form: the image card carries a voice-opening that ends in "…", and the caption continues below.
- The gap between them is the third dimension, a pause he crosses with his thumb or simply by waiting.

**M9. The composition rule (from "never the same grain, lens or pairing twice in succession")**
- Becomes the Feed's invisible ordering engine.
- Arrivals rotate the speaking being (him, cloth, land, house, maker, customer, machine) and the pairing:
  - *need underneath* (Arch with Gaia);
  - *what holds* (Arch with Sid);
  - *the whole architecture* (Arch with Sakshi).
- The rotation is never named on screen.

**M10. Signal fidelity (from "uncertain → nothing; silence holds")**
- An authoring law for every other-being story.
- If a voice cannot be grounded in the source, it is not written. The slot stays as white space in the room.

**M11. Silence endings (from the Book of Makers' silence typology)**
- Recipient, biological, privacy, domestic, normalcy, founding, pre-arrival and inexhaustible silences become a vocabulary of *ways a story ends*.
- Some stories end on what was never said. Each silence has its own fade.

**M12. The quiet ones (from the emergence voice-state ladder)**
- The ladder: before-state, contact, declaration, the telling, the quiet ones.
- A story that gets *shorter* each time he returns (by dwell) until only the landing line is left: "the more complete the system, the quieter the arrival".
- This is the mirror of the existing signature "layers on return by dwell". Both should exist.

**M13. Faces of grace as an Archive index**
- The faces: Scraps, Hidden One, Without You There, Wash, Declaration, Meets You There, At First Touch, Catch, Release, Whole Surface, Yours Too, Right Through.
- They become one way of browsing the Archive book: by the *face* in which grace arrived, not by room.
- The faces are named only in the index, never inside a story.

**M14. Recitation (from the Book of Makers' closing recitation of names)**
- A Rite: a slow litany of every story title he has received, read in order, one per breath.
- No names, no counts. It closes with a line honouring the stories that are still unwritten.

**M15. Six layers by dwell (from SupAbsorb's discovery that experiences need six passes: Gaia, Sid, Arch, Sakshi, Karishma, Bindu)**
- A long-dwelt story shows its six layers in that order: the need, what holds, the voice, the witness, the grace, the dot.
- They appear as faint marginal lines, one by one.

**M16. Honesty mark (from the LPE's ◇ marker)**
- A tiny unlabelled glyph before the sentence where a being says what it *cannot* do.
- Honest limits are where trust is born (seed 89).

**M17. Earned warmth (from the LPE's zone map)**
- A room's palette warms subtly as he spends unhurried time in it.
- Never counted, never reversed, never shown as progress.

**M18. Crossing texture (from the LPE's departure toast)**
- When a story moves into another being's register (him to land, or cloth to machine), the *texture* changes: paper to linen, linen to concrete.
- No label, keeping the architecture invisible.

**M19. The duet (from the brand-partner voice field)**
- Two beings speak across one seam: the outside cloth and the inside lining, the grower and the returner.
- Tilting the phone moves between their two voices.

**M20. The Finder (from the Discovery System's seven dimensions)**
- He can ask the Feed by *function* (something that releases, holds, returns, catches, stretches and recovers) and receive a story.
- The cloth's own verbs become the search, in the shape of the Sakshi seeds (present-tense, two to four words: "the stretch recovers").

**M21. The Tree (from roots, trunk and canopy)**
- An Archive view:
  - **roots:** the land, the house, the machine;
  - **trunk:** his Codex stories;
  - **canopy:** folded leaves, which are stories not yet written.
- A new Codex entry unfolds a leaf.

- The Postface's lines.
- The Sakshi-seed taglines as story text. M20 uses only their grammatical shape.
- The founding contamination story's company, year, figures and agency. Only the shape survives: a poisoned ground and a refusal (seeds 14, 90).
- All SupAbsorb product codes, capacities, prices, compliance and industry names, and every competitor.
- The predecessor-owner story beyond "a name people warned each other about" (seed 27).
- The family etymology chain and its private names. Seed 13 keeps the shape without the surname.
- The arrival date and order of the pairing of Ashrey and Karishma.

**Pandemic:** the pandemic is never named. Use "a week when everything else was closing" (seeds 31, 70).

**Humanitarian:** chapters 1 and 9 are used only as abstractions. No girls, schools, countries or charities.

## 6. EMERGENT: my own concepts

**E1. The Ground, as the floor under the whole app.**
- The Ground's stories don't live only in their room. Their single dark drop sits under the last line of *any* story on a low-light evening.
- If he lingers, it is taken in, and a land voice speaks one sentence before the page clears.
- **How it feels:** the planet quietly finishing his sentences.

**E2. The Stack Night.**
- One night told through five layers, pressed into by depth.
- The sleeper's layer is blank, and on reaching it the screen dims to his own reflection.
- **How it feels:** sinking through a mattress into himself.

**E3. The Unwritten room.**
- A wall of cards, each showing only a landing line.
- Stillness fills in the story; movement erases it.
- **How it feels:** a room where the evidence is that nothing went wrong, and he is the one who notices.

**E4. The year of veils.**
- Mirror pages arrive across a year, walking the inner arc from Permanence to Return.
- On the last one the role is only "?" and the left page is his own dark-glass reflection.
- **How it feels:** being named twelve times and then un-named.

**E5. Bindu before / bindu after.**
- The app's first-ever sentence returns as its farthest sentence.
- **How it feels:** finding the front door from the inside.

**E6. Second Letters.**
- New Codex entries don't only become new stories. Some write a two-line coda to an *old* story: what that moment became a year later.
- **How it feels:** the Feed itself ageing well, like a second review.

**E7. The Swatch.**
- Rarely, folded at the bottom of a received story, a three-line story he didn't "order" appears: a tiny tucked extra from another being.
- No notification, never predictable.
- **How it feels:** the surprise of arrival living inside the page, like an unasked-for square in a parcel.

**E8. The Recitation Rite.**
- A weekly or seasonal rite: every received title, read slowly, one per breath, ending on the unwritten.
- **How it feels:** hearing his own life read back as one long name.

**E9. Triptychs of One Life.**
- A single moment returns three times from three beings: maker, cloth, place.
- The Archive binds them.
- **How it feels:** walking around a sculpture.

**E10. The Finder by verb.**
- He asks for a function, not a topic ("something that lets go", "something that holds where I can't"), and the Feed answers with a story whose being does that.
- **How it feels:** going to a fabric shelf for the self.

**E11. The Duet tilt.**
- Two beings across one seam. Tilting left gives the outside voice, tilting right gives the inside voice, and level gives both lines at once.
- **How it feels:** holding a two-sided cloth up to the light.

**E12. Honest limits as a signature of trust.**
- Every other-being story may include one sentence where the being says what it cannot do, marked only by a tiny diamond.
- Over time he notices the diamonds are where he trusted the story most.
- **How it feels:** being leveled with.

**E13. The Tree of the Feed.**
- An Archive view in which roots (land, house, machine), trunk (his memos) and canopy (folded, unwritten leaves) grow.
- A new memo unfolds a leaf.
- **How it feels:** tending something that was already growing.

**E14. The completed yard.**
- Stories about things that are finished (the last of a kind, the empty drawer, the cloth that needs no tending) carry a quiet completed state.
- Once received, their page shows "every yard that will ever exist has been made" in the being's own words. Completion is shown as beauty, not loss.
- **How it feels:** a door closing with everything inside.

**E15. The earth-keeper's chapter, written by the Feed.**
- The story book's own pending chapter for the earth-keeping arm has never been written.
- Let the Ground room *be* that chapter, assembled one land voice at a time inside the Instrument, with the house's own pending inscription withheld.
- **How it feels:** the Feed completing the book that the Postface said it was always part of.
