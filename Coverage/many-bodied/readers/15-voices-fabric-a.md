## 1. How rhyme is SHOWN (a shared vocabulary for every seed below; nothing here locks)

All of these are always visible. Dwell deepens them. None depends on having received the host story.

- **Selvedge.** A thin finished edge along the host story's margin carries the rhyming story's title in small type, the way a bolt's selvedge carries its mill mark. Touch or dwell and it opens beside the host.
- **Reverse.** The cloth story is printed on the back of the host page. A two-finger turn, or a still hold, shows the other face. This fits A Maya Game's two-sided grammar, and it now works in any room where the rhyme is a "same yarn, other face" rhyme.
- **Lit word.** One word glows faintly in both stories at once (*already*, *quiet*, *through*, *both*). Wherever that word appears in the Feed, it carries the same low light.
- **Weft.** In the Codex/Present river, a fine horizontal thread runs between the host memo's bead and the cloth story's bead. It is seen from any distance and never needs a tap.
- **Laid beside.** On the room's shelf, the two stories rest folded together as a pair. A fold of the second shows under the first.
- **Echo line.** After the host story's landing line, the cloth story's opening image appears at a fifth of the brightness and fades in with dwell. It is a doorway that is already open.
- **Shared verb.** Both stories carry the same seed verb on their last line (see the Verb Mala, §3).

## 3. Corpus-level structures that are already game-shaped

1. **The 7+2 arc as a reception path** (SKILL.md). The steps are Exhausted Belief → Failed Others → Arrival → Threshold → Grace Space → Faces → Silence, plus the Seed and the Gaia Inversion. Any Feed story, including the Codex memos, can be read through it. The Threshold step is "the thing you must do before you can know". In the Feed that becomes the room's gesture (hold, turn, wash). It is never a lock: the grace is visible on both sides of it.

2. **The Sakshi Seed's two readings** (generation-protocol Phase 3). The test is: "does this spec read differently before the journey and after?" As a mechanic, every cloth story ends on a plain line ("The peel is quiet.") that, after dwell on the story, settles into italics with one word lit. The words stay the same and the meaning turns.

3. **The Verb Mala** (series-index, Sakshi Seed Verb). There are 58 verbs in sequence, a natural strand of beads: *Hold → Bond → Be → Let through … → Breathes → Catches → Is → Absorbs → Stays → Cools → Touches → Grew → Flies … → Vanishes → Graces → Performs.* The verbs recur (hold ×4, wicks ×4, cools ×4), and every recurrence carries a different meaning. In the corpus's own words: "the verb pool is finite; architecture divergence is what matters." That is a rhyme engine, not a counter.

4. **The Grace Surfacing chain** (series-index): *Hidden inside → Underneath → On the surface → … → In the forgetting → In the partnership → In the recognition.* There are 56 positions of where grace lives. They make a ready-made mandala or wheel of locations, and each Feed story could sit at one.

5. **The Karishma Operation chain:** *Hidden → Dual → Comfort → Vanishing … → Simplification as grace → Recognition as performance.* It is a second wheel, naming how grace works rather than where.

6. **Faces of Grace are simultaneous, not sequential** (Phase 4.4: "if it's a sequence, you have one face with phases"). Each fabric shows 3–5 faces at once. A story could be a turnable prism, with all faces present and one facing you.

7. **Three depths for every community:** Full Story, Vignette, Silence, and "Silence is a complete state" (Locked Decision 3). This maps one-to-one onto layers that appear by dwell. A story can be held as a silence (title only), a vignette (one moment) or a full arc, and each is complete.

8. **The harvest:** voices drift to the page they belong to (`harvest-registry.md`, "bidirectional and multi-cycle; each pass cleans its own corpus and enriches its siblings"). It is a living migration between rooms.

9. **Substrate → laminate verb pairs.** The same being takes a new verb when a missing layer joins it: *comes through → absorbs*, *pills → holds*, *flows → vanishes*, *shelters → graces*, *recovers → fits*. In the Feed, a story can have a "before its backing" voice and an "after its backing" voice.

10. **The Stack.** Liner, core and shell are three cloths in one product, and "each fabric's story describes its role in the stack". Register descends with depth: the surface declares, the core confirms quietly. The humanitarian stack is three cloths for one girl. This is a triptych story form.

11. **Domain questions.** Each of the 26 communities carries a question it asks of any seed:
    - "Does this work when infrastructure disappears?"
    - "Does it reward the courage of trying?"
    - "Does it answer doubt on first contact?"
    - "Does it work in a context nobody planned for?"
    - "Does it do something different from what it was designed for?"
    - "Does it match the urgency of the need?"

    These make a deck of lenses that can be laid over any Codex story. This is the Gaia Inversion made playable.

12. **The Failed Others taxonomy.** Ten types plus about twenty sub-expressions, among them sound, face, aesthetic category, respectful succession, systemic paradigm, self, expectation and benchmark. Together they are a cast of honourable predecessors.

13. **Pronoun architecture.** Level 1 is spoken to the cloth ("you", Karishma above). Level 2 is about the maker (she/he). The self-katha is the cloth as "I". One being, three pronouns, three distances.

14. **Seeds arrive rather than being found.** The series-index records seeds "delivered, not directed", given by Ashrey while doing other things ("The Pointe Wicks", "The wool flies", "The Mesh Cools?"). That is a co-creation ritual: the instrument offers the specification, and the verb arrives in his voice.

15. **The story count falls to zero.** The last 28 fabrics in a row produced no full maker stories ("Forty-second zero-story fabric"). The corpus itself is moving toward silence as its mature form. The Feed can honour that with a room where the stories are only names.

## 4. New rooms and forms the cloth asks for

- **The Wash** (room). Grammar: *renewal, not repetition*. A story here is the same text each time, but its surface softens by dwell (the Bloom). Harsh phrasing relaxes into the bloomed phrasing as you stay; nothing is gained by revisiting. Signature: "the many-hundredth time is the first." Holds 6, 17, 26, 36, 61, 62.
- **The Unwritten** (room). Grammar: *the title is the story*. Only names of things made: a bee suit, a robe for a grandchild, thirteen projects, codex titles with no memo body. Dwell brings no text, only a deeper stillness and the selvedge lines to stories that rhyme. Silence counts as a complete state, not a gap. Holds 41, 78, and every silence territory in the 14 folders.
- **The Ones Before** (room). Grammar: *thank the predecessor*. The Failed Others speak: the loud loop, the coating, the fast absorber, the plastic wrap, the old liner.
