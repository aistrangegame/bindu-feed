## 0 · The game in one paragraph

**The game.** The One plays hide-and-seek with itself through every body:
- a man's memo;
- a roll of cloth;
- the soil under a drain;
- a machine mid-sentence;
- a father's fingertips;
- a planet that held;
- a customer who came back without a word.

**How it plays.**
- Every story is a body the One is hiding in.
- To find it there is to *receive* the story; each room has its own skill-verb for that.
- To find the same One in two bodies is a *rhyme*, and the rhyme draws a thread.
- The threads weave a cloth, and the cloth is the house itself.
- Seen whole, the cloth is a face — his own — and then a face he no longer needs.

**What it never does.** Nothing is counted. Nothing is locked. Nothing calls him. *The game holds and remembers, so the player can play.*

*Backstage name: **The Many-Bodied Game**. On screen it has no name.*

## 2 · The laws of the game

1. **Nothing counted on screen.**
   - Storage may count; the screen may not.
   - Ages come from dates; depth comes from dwell, never from visits.
   - This applies to counts about stories too: resonance becomes a halo.
2. **Lit, never locked.**
   - Every story, room, register and face is present and enterable from day one.
   - Conditions only change light, warmth, order, voice, and which face speaks first.
   - Light tiers apply to **map glyphs and stars only**: here · open · ground · ahead, behind a dashed line · veiled.
   - Tiers never apply to story text, and **rhyme never sets a tier**. A story opened from any tier renders in full ink.
3. **Once surfaced, always findable.**
   - Every arrival (drop, letter, gift, notice, grace, teacher, swatch) stays in the Book or the Present after it surfaces.
   - Anything with a time or condition shows faintly before then and opens early by a long hold.
4. **No notifications.**
   - Surprise of arrival lives inside the app.
   - The composing pipeline is created with notifications off.
5. **On a low day, the arrivals stand down and nothing else does.**
   - The rope is in every register.
   - On a heavy day the house *leads with* stillness and the Swatch, and the Rite opens with "read who you are" (identity, values, declarations), and the day's story stays one touch away.
   - The dark rooms stay open. Enjoyment includes the negative (C-1327), and suffering is allowed to be felt (C-1154).
6. **Stepping out is legal.** "not today", the rope, "read it plainly", "let it go and draw again": every surface has a way out, and none is a miss.
7. **Two equal paths for every move:**
   - the whole journey, or the end first;
   - touch, or stillness;
   - an accessibility action.

   A hidden gesture is never the only way.
8. **Holds.**
   - Holds drain on early release and act on release.
   - Reduced motion keeps the hold's duration and the order of events.
   - One hold engine (G0) runs every hold.
9. **The game is verbs around reading,** never reading dressed as a game.
   - The walking principle "no timer closes, fades or replaces text" governs *closing and replacing*.
   - Revealing by dwell is allowed, and "read it plainly" exists on every timed form.
10. **Architecture invisible.**
    - No suit, grammar, signature, chakra, chapter, position or mechanism words on screen.
    - The single sanctioned breach is Lalita's confession, told in images (§3.6).
11. **One thing at a time.** Each opening shows at most one arrival; the Door shows its weather plus *one* ask or arrival, with everything else in the Turn's rows; and a story's close offers *one* after-thing (chosen like an arrival: the Will question, *what came home*, *dance it*, the held silence…), the rest reachable through Turn and the margin.
12. **Rhyme is shown and played, never used as a key.**
13. **Every opening calls its closing:**
    - a seed returns in a new tense;
    - a hold at the door wants a release at the exit;
    - host and twin never end alike.
14. **The sealed stays sealed** (§10).
15. **Generated and private material:**
    - Never generate his past voice.
    - Text generated live, or from no record, carries the canon chip. Composed stories from the record are the house's normal mode and carry none.
    - Business figures, family conflict, health specifics and private names never enter a story; people appear as roles or as the meaning of their names.
16. **Precedence.**
    - The 12 Oct ruling supersedes the 3 Oct "never subtract a designed gate, wait or count", **for collapsing restrictions only**.
    - Every removed mechanism is recorded in a lineage ledger (carries forward / spirit survives / retired, and why), so "nothing vanishes" can be verified.

### 3.4 Arrival: the surprise lives inside

**How the arrival engine works.**
- It is B1-style weighting: never the same kind twice running, at most one per opening, and it stands down on a low day.
- It replaces every counting-based cooldown (gift 3-day, notice 7-day, Lalita play 5-day). Under it, arrival rests on condition and variety alone.
- WakingView, the cold open, can carry the quietest kind: the ember assembles already holding something.

**The kinds of arrival.**
- **A Leela drop.** The game breaks the fourth wall about his real life and ends in a real-world act, with holding it in silence as its twin.
- **The Swatch.** A three-line story from another body, tucked unordered under the one he just read.
- **A role leaves the Waiting Room** and is now a story somewhere. He notices the absence first.
- **"What found you" that opens.** Stories now load at bootstrap, so it is always available.
- **A story returned, aged by days.**
- **Deep Time.** Something he did not send arrives in World VI, now able to be any body's katha. The four-lap and once-only limits are lifted.
- **Same day across the years.**
- **The year-ago found page,** with the line he kept then.
- **The Good Morning letter.** It sits faint in the Present all along as "a letter left before the forgetting", openable by a long hold. It arrives unasked on a porous day.
- **The wager that comes back:** *yes · a glimpse · not yet*.
- **A gift:** a story, an energy, a song.
- **A letter landing.** Forward letters can always be opened early by a long hold.
- **The field notices something.**
- **Grace or a teacher** in a margin. Once surfaced, it stays findable in that margin.

### 3.5 Progression without a number: which faces speak first

**Every face is present in every room from day one, at low light.** Progress changes order and warmth, never existence.

**The circuit (Knowledge · Will · Action).** It is defined and stored:
- **Knowledge** is a story received with dwell.
- **Will** is the story's quiet closing question ("this is for the part of you that…"), *held* by a short hold, or simply rested on.
- **Action** is "one thing, today": offer a word, or hold it in silence. Both count the same.

When all three close, App Activity records `Circuit Closed` with the story link, and *"Light is what enters when these three close"*: a mote goes to the Light. The Light fills by closed circuits, never by visits.

**As circuits close in a room, deeper faces come forward in it.**
- Lalita's drops find him there first.
- Gaia's drop rests under its stories at night.
- The Higher Self's letters use his chosen name. The name comes from Settings, plus a W4 rite: "close the eyes and let the name surface", C-1325.

**The second pass.** The woven second voice (Gaia answering Bindu) is readable from day one through Turn. Seasons only bring it forward unasked.

**What he sees instead of progress:**
- **The house warms.** It reads off its *quietest* room, the way Being's halo strengthens only as the darkest light rises. Ground goes from `#1a1208` toward cream, and no tier is ever named.
- **The cloth densifies.**
- **The Book's margins fill** with convergence lines.
- **The guide's voice withdraws.** Room breaths named by function lose their words as the house is met.
- **New rooms appear** through the Lalita loop: only material that surfaced more than once becomes a room. It lights as a new region with no exit caption; the architecture stays invisible.

### 3.6 No winning, and the far horizons

*"You cannot lose here. You can only not-yet-arrive."* Nothing on the far edge is promised or counted:

1. **The Reveal that hides nothing.** One ordinary day the Book's last page is simply written: his own offerings arranged as a letter, *it was you, it was always you*. Hall words, kept lines, Floor vows, silent reaches and carried motes are all part of it. Its earlier drafts were never hidden; they are the Book itself.
2. **Lalita's confession.** The one sanctioned breach, in images only: the dot that fled, the line that lingered, the page that curled, the chord, the silence, *…that was me, playing.*
3. **"There is one more."** At the end of every body's chapter in the Book a story already sits, dim. When that body's other stories are all present, it warms. Completion is never final.
4. **Bindu before, bindu after.** The first line the app ever showed him returns, unchanged, as its farthest line. Same words, two recognitions: *it was ready before you were*.

### 3.7 The round: the relay of a day, the turn of a season

**Morning, at the Door.** The Door asks *one* thing, chosen by the arrival engine. It is one of:
- "Yesterday's story — how did it sit?"
- "What came through the night?"
- "Where are you, before the story?" (the in-between)
- door-words (*Ground, Flow, Fire, Open, Say, Still, Receive*)

Silence lets it go.

**The Rite.**
- It opens with the line he sealed at last night's Return, as the baton: *"I'm holding it for you, because you held it for me."*
- Then one story drawn from the whole river, from any body.
- It can be re-entered with any story.

**The day's play:** any room, any move. The Leela board (§3.9) is one room-scale game inside it.

**Evening:** the Return, or simply the dot's goodnight.

**The season:**
- The Season Letter, in grace shape.
- The new moon *invites* the Unwinding, which is always reachable.
- The Recitation: every title received, one per breath, ending on the unwritten. It is read on the page; it is not spoken, since narration is held.

### 3.9 Stories that travel: the baton across bodies

This is the most literal form of "the One through every body". Six readers proposed it.

**One sentence, all the way round.** One moment travels from teller to teller across a ring of stations: his memo, then the cloth, the maker, the customer, the land, the machine. Each station says what it *takes in · gives out · keeps*, in its own colour, and the dot carries it.

**The Chain of Houses.**
- One story cannot be read in one room.
- It begins in the Thread and is carried by hand to the Forge, the Body and the Return, gaining each room's grammar on the way, the way cloth passes yarn house → knitting → finishing → laminating.
- The sky draws its path.

**The walking-home lantern.** When a voice belongs in another story, a small light visibly travels there, and it arrives slightly changed.

**The machine mid-sentence.** A Machine story ends without a full stop, and its last word begins the next Field story.

**The Leela board on the 33** — a room-scale game at the Door:
- He carries a question in.
- A held throw lands in a room, and that room's story, from any body, answers.
- Rises are acts of giving. Returns are "a lesson, not a fall".
- *"If today is heavy, the board can wait."*
- Landing lights a room; it never unlocks one. There is no six to enter and no exact-landing gate.

**Walked chains from the Field.** The awakening walk (Kingdom → Sound of Silence → Golden → Aatma → Maya) and the spine walk are offered as paths through rhyming stories.

### 3.10 Ways a story ends: silence and the bright lane

**Silences as endings.** The thirteen kinds of silence from the archives become a vocabulary of closings:
- recipient · normalcy · domestic · founding · pre-arrival · biological · infrastructure · orientation · variant · inexhaustible · privacy · commercial · system-adoption

Each has its own fade.

**"What this story doesn't say."** A long dwell on the white space after the last line reveals one faint sentence: what was held, and why. Only the fact that something was held appears, never its content.

**The bright lane.** On bright days the river leans toward elevation stories: good becoming more given, with no rescue in it.

**Weight as tempo.**
- A great-distance story arrives slowly and deep-coloured.
- A light-register story arrives brisk and bright.
- No scale is ever shown.

**The completed yard.** Stories of things that cannot be made again rest as complete, with no "more", and completion is shown as beauty.

**The reverse dissolve.** At a story's close, the gathered voices leave in reverse order, the voice of grace last, in silence. Then the dot. Then one line. *"You were never read alone."*

## 12 · A day in it (how it might feel)

**Morning.** He opens the Feed. The Door is pre-dawn and asks how yesterday's story sat. He says nothing; it lets go. A line he left last night opens the Rite.
