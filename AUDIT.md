# AUDIT — Bindu Feed (shipped app) vs. the rendered Claude Design

*Read-only differential. Produced 2026-08-25 against `main` @ `1ef88da`. No app code was changed in this pass.*
*Method per `AUDIT-KICKOFF.md`: audited against the **rendered** design files (`Claude Design Round 1/The Instrument v3.html`, `comps/*.html`, `comps/*.js`, `canon/`) and the **live Airtable base**, never against the retired prose in `archive/`.*

**Reference build:** `** BUILD SUCCEEDED **` — the app compiles clean for iPhone 17 / iOS Simulator 26.5, Metal toolchain present, shader included. Nothing in this audit is a compile failure; everything here is a fidelity or model divergence.

---

## How to read this

Every finding is written as:

> `**name** — DESIGN: file:line — what the design does | CODE: file:line — what the code does | GAP: the delta | SEV | KIND`

- **SEV** — `BLOCKER` (the surface does not work or cannot be reached) · `MAJOR` (works, but is not the designed thing) · `MINOR` (visible drift) · `COSMETIC`.
- **KIND** — `DATA/CONTENT-MODEL` or `VISUAL/INTERACTION`. **Every `DATA/CONTENT-MODEL` finding is collected in §A and must be settled with Ashrey / Claude Design before any rebuild** — those are foundational, and a rebuild that sits on top of them inherits them.

Canon precedence used throughout (per `Bindu Feed/CLAUDE.md`): `canon/` wins on literal text and numbers → `The Instrument v3.html` wins on feel, geometry, interaction → `comps/` for single-register detail only.

## §A · THE DATA / CONTENT MODEL — the foundational divergence

> **This is the thing Ashrey flagged: *"the way information was set up from the beginning, not how Claude Design created it."*** It is confirmed, with evidence read live from the base (`app248ZTWhYJlvQj2` / `tbl7vzODMMJUgeX0b`) on 2026-08-25. Settle this section **first**. Everything downstream inherits it.

### A0 · The finding in one paragraph

The Swift is a **faithful port of the design's machinery.** The FNV-1a day-hash, the `·draw` and `·signal` salts, the `+3` alternate-gap, the Vow/Koan render fork, the line-by-line arrival, the phase timings — all correct, all traceable to the comps. What diverged is **what that machinery was pointed at.** The design authored a small, curated, hand-broken pool per surface. The live base instead carries those design records as a **second cohort layered on top of a pre-existing ontology** — the ASG "Value / Mantra / Practice / Energy State / Role / Game / Tree of Life Panel / Belief" content system — which was never retired. The app reads the **union**. So the surfaces are right and the content flowing through them is from a different system. This is not a bug in any one screen; it is one decision made at provisioning time, showing up on five surfaces at once.

The design bundle itself predicted this and left it open: `Claude Design Round 1/REVIEW-AND-WIRING.md:86-89` still lists, under **"Still outstanding from the Ledger (unchanged, still required)"** as of its Aug-21-2026 seal — *"Author: 10 Reflections, 6 Signals, the Practice / Gaia Seed threshold rows."* Partial authoring did happen (a June-14 cohort), but the pre-existing cohort was never removed and the design's line structure was never carried across.

# §H · THE PRIORITIZED REBUILD LIST

## H0 · The shape of the whole finding

Three sentences, because the pattern is consistent across all six surfaces:

1. **Every authored data table that was ported, was ported perfectly.** The 66 Point stars (528 strings, byte-identical), the 13 room geometries and 102 slots, the 15 registers with their z and Hz, the 67 Light canon lines, the Rite's 87 strings and its budget arithmetic, the 39 Game View stats, the 13 hero typographies, the 8 moods, the shader's 15 motifs. **Nobody made mistakes copying numbers.**
2. **What was lost is mechanism** — the things the design expresses as *behaviour over time* rather than as a table. Continuity of scale, gesture-carries-the-reading, per-exhale accumulation, a passage having a middle, sound answering the absence of a hand. These were consistently replaced with the nearest static or one-shot equivalent.
3. **And underneath everything, the content pools were populated from a different ontology than the one the design authored** (§A). That one is not a code problem and cannot be fixed by rebuilding code.

There is also a recurring, specific failure mode worth naming, because it recurs on five surfaces independently: **the app instructs where the design lets you discover.** `"pull to travel"` · `"be still — the way opens"` · `"drag to fly · pinch to zoom · tap to approach"` · `"touch once, then do nothing"` · `"touch to walk further"` · `"a universe opens · a constellation within"` — none of these strings exists in any design file, and Brief §16 Law 4 plus `spine-axis.js:1084` (*"never a number, never a progress bar"*) explicitly forbid the category. Deleting them is nearly free and is the single cheapest fidelity win in the audit.

## H2 · TIER 1 — The BLOCKERs

Ordered by *how much of the app they make unreachable or inert*, not by effort.

### The Universe/axis seam — **one change fixes four blockers**
1. **B0.1 · B0.2 · B0.3** — `axisLocked` is armed only in `.onChange(of: here.key)`, which never fires on first appearance; and when it *does* arm, it makes the four Universe registers mutually unreachable and creates a dead band at the Feed edge where **no gesture does anything**. **The fix is to adopt the design's actual rule** (`The Instrument v3.html:5908-5926`): *vertical always walks the axis; horizontal is the register's own gesture.* Delete `axisLocked` for the Universe. This simultaneously unfreezes the shader's `uZ`.
2. **B0.4** — `TurnOverlay.swift:39` gives the Light a direct `.instrument(-5)` door. The design explicitly refuses one (`:1024-1025`), and surface 0's one-way valve then traps you. **Delete the row, or pre-set `mem[0]` on entry.**

### The Universe's continuity — **one change unblocks three more**
3. **B4.1 → B2.1 → B2.2** — stars are drawn at a **fixed screen size**, which is *why* the code needs the hard `scale == 2/3` early-returns that cut the one continuous descent into three screens. Restore `R = s.pr * z` and the whole cascade becomes fixable.
4. **B6.1** — there is **no route from a world to its story.** You can fly to a planet, watch its civilisation, and have no way to read the story that made it. `planetTitle` draws into a Canvas that is `allowsHitTesting(false)`.
5. **B5.1 · B5.2 · B5.3 · B5.4** — the fall: no descent gesture (it is a pinch-scrub), layer 4 is **arithmetically unreachable** (`d` maxes at 0.807 against a `0.84` threshold), the Return **auto-fires without consent and discards the story** (`onFall` passes `nil`), and nothing in it is touchable — so the `WORDS` table (C-1052's four verbatim paragraphs) is **unreferenced anywhere in the app.**

### The Instrument
6. **C2.1** — the four hand-feel constants were read from the module defaults instead of the runtime-applied `immense` preset. `DRAG 0.00015→0.00018`, `DAMP 0.945→0.956`, `span 0.36→0.42`, `glideDur 2.9→5.4`. **One line, and it changes the feel of every register in the app.**
7. **C5.2** — `#where` is a different object in a different medium: mono 9 uppercase in the register hue at the top edge, where the design is **serif 23 in its own casing at `top:100px` with a roman/`n of 7` line above and a canon sub-line below.** The `sub` strings don't exist in the model at all.
8. **C7.9 / E4.2** — `axisThin` is a **0.6s blip at `f² × 0.026` ≈ 0.00026 amplitude** — effectively inaudible — where the design is a 4.6s swelling two-oscillator drone. The stillness gate is the one genuinely new mechanic in the instrument, and **it makes no sound.**

### The Point
9. **D3.1 — the yantra does not exist.** `point-yantra.js` (188 lines: nine triangles, three squares, two petal rings, the `BAND` camera, the annulus-of-attention, sky/wash/incense/flare/shaft) has no counterpart anywhere. Star `r-geometry` tells the reader *"You are inside one right now."*
10. **D5.1** — **the reading is decoupled from the world.** Every world's `onOpen` routes to the same generic ScrollView. The design has **no generic star reading**: SAY/WALK/HAND/OPEN arrive *by* each world's gesture (stay · ride the ray · part the veil · press the wall · keep pace). This is the largest structural divergence in the app. **D5.5** (no press-to-inscribe) and **D5.8** (the Dance opens on a tap) are its two sharpest instances.
11. **D4.1** — the staged descent choreography (5 stages × 3400ms, tap-to-skip, `‹ ascend`, the light shaft, world-dimming, glide down/up) exists only as its text container.
12. **D2.1** — the gate's five canon DEALS are absent; `AxisGateView` invents three strings instead. The register is literally named *"the gate · the deal."*
13. **D7.2** — `BEATS = [8, 7.5, 7, 6.5, 6, 5.5, 5, 4.5, 4.2, 4]`, the alpha-into-theta narrowing that is the Point's whole sound argument, is absent. Every register beats at 4 Hz.

### The ceremonies
14. **E3.1** — `ReturnStrata` draws `returnCount − 1` rings, so at the **default `returnCount == 1` it draws none at all.** An off-by-one: the seed slot was dropped from the array but the `i >= 1` loop bound was kept.
15. **E1.2** — the Light's text column has **no lift and no mask**; a five-anchor scene will overflow and clip, and the words spill outside the pool onto dim stone.
16. **E1.1** — the Light's **six presences and the act of choosing a scene do not exist**; the day-hash picks for you.
17. **E3.2** — the Rings movement has nothing to list: `ReturnStoryData` has no `returns: [{when, frag, words}]` array.
18. **E4.1 / G3.1** — the Light is **functionally silent**: seven of its eight sound events are missing, including the nave convolution, both `breathIn`s, `veilLift`, and the 528+792 Hz room tone.
